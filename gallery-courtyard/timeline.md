# Courtyard Timeline Wall

- 2020: Pen-and-ink sketches of Mount Rainier from the beach at Denny Blaine, then watercolor to catch the luminosity of the sky light.
- 2022: Bigger work, in watercolor and watercolor pastel.
- 2022: First trip to El Salvador, and three sunsets painted at Atami.
- 2022: El Salvador: Past, Present and Future, painted after that first trip.
- 2022: Portraits of Bitcoin conference speakers begin as a conversation starter; 20 or 30 handed out so far.
- 2023: Back to El Salvador, and a sunset in El Zonte.
- 2023: First ocean-going kayak trip, out to Sucia and Patos.
- 2023: First art show, at Sid's Studio 601 in Seattle, with an auction of Ukraine paintings for a Ukrainian family.
- 2024: Group show at Adopting Bitcoin El Salvador.
- 2025: Solo show at Adopting Bitcoin Cape Town, and a hike up Table Mountain.
- 2025: Solo show at Victrola Coffee Shop, Seattle.
- November 2025: Community mural in Berlín, El Salvador, at the Bitcoin Festival.
- 2026: Coined in Watercolor premieres at the Bitcoin Film Festival in Warsaw.
- 2026: Friends Club festival mural, a 40 by 9 foot shipping container in Lillooet, BC.
