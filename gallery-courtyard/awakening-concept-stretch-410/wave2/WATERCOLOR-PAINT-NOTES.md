# Awakening — Paint-First Watercolor Notes (#410 wave2) — PRIVATE
Lock unchanged: **#3 → #11 → #12 → #17**, plus **11b Lamps in a Ripple**. Agent-proposed stretch energy; optimistic, self-sovereign, waking up. Arches 300 lb cold press throughout; watercolor + watercolor pastel accents on dry layers.
**Series palette spine:** Neutral Tint, Ultramarine, Phthalo Turquoise, Quinacridone Rose, New Gamboge, Cadmium Orange, Dioxazine Violet.
**Repeating motif rule — the luminous eye/hand/light:** it recurs across the set but never straight reuse. Each piece shifts angle, light, and emotion (below).

## #3 Mirror of Every Headline — I See the Worst on That Screen
- **Paper/size:** Quarter sheet, tight crop.
- **Palette:** Neutral Tint + Dioxazine Violet (shards), Ultramarine; one Phthalo Turquoise iris left luminous.
- **Light:** Cold rain-window side light, low key.
- **Technique:** Glaze shards in 3–4 transparent layers, drying between, hard edges; reserve the eye whites and lift a catchlight. Storm/smoke fragments wet-in-wet inside individual shards only.
- **Angle/emotion step 1:** straight-on, close — steadiness under pressure.
- **Avoid:** recognizable logos, faces of real people, paranoid wording or iconography.

## #11 The Machine Offers Its Hand — Partners at the Unfinished Sketch
- **Paper/size:** Half sheet horizontal.
- **Palette:** Quinacridone Rose + New Gamboge (human hand), pearlescent glazes from Phthalo Turquoise + Ultramarine thinned to whisper (machine hand), sketch in loose rainbow.
- **Light:** Side light from right, cooler than #3 but warming.
- **Technique:** Machine hand mostly reserved white with reflected colors from the sketch glazed in — it borrows the painting's color, not chrome. Optional iridescent watercolor in a single stroke.
- **Angle/emotion step 2:** profile, mid-close — curiosity, edge.
- **Avoid:** sci-fi chrome, red eyes, menace.

## 11b Lamps in a Ripple
- **Paper/size:** Half sheet horizontal.
- **Palette:** Indigo-leaning Ultramarine + Dioxazine Violet sky; New Gamboge → Cadmium Orange lamps; Neutral Tint ash.
- **Light:** Dusk, warmth travelling left to right.
- **Technique:** Wax-resist lamp halos before the sky; graded saturation so early lamps glow brighter (the ripple). Dragon ash as a single soft grey bloom at margin.
- **Angle/emotion step 3:** slightly elevated — care, community.

## #12 Seeing the Worst, Staying Anyway — I Choose You Anyway
- **Paper/size:** Half sheet vertical.
- **Palette:** Quinacridone Rose + Phthalo Turquoise neon reflections, Cadmium Orange umbrella interior, Neutral Tint street.
- **Light:** Evening after storm; warm dome of the umbrella vs cool street.
- **Technique:** Street reflections pulled down wet-in-wet with a tilted board; umbrella glazed warm last so it glows. Tear on the cheek: one lifted line.
- **Angle/emotion step 4:** eye level, two figures — tenderness. The luminous eye from #3 returns, now looking at another person.

## #17 Self-Portrait, Turned Toward You — Better Than You Think
- **Paper/size:** Full sheet vertical (the largest — finale).
- **Palette:** Full spine at full saturation; palette wells in foreground as jewel dots.
- **Light:** Warm north window turned gold.
- **Technique:** Background collage of earlier pieces as ghosted washes (#3 shards, #11 hands, lamps) — reinterpreted, not copied. Face in 2–3 confident layers; brush offered toward viewer with foreshortening.
- **Angle/emotion step 5:** 3/4 turned outward — invitation.
- **Avoid:** cartoon proportions, heroic poster pose.

## Progression summary
Cold → warm; close/straight → profile → elevated → eye-level pair → turned to viewer; steadiness → curiosity → care → tenderness → invitation.
