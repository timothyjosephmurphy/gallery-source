# Awakening — Wave2 Subjects (#410) — PRIVATE
5 new briefs; no duplication of wave1 #1–#17, 5b, 11b. Numbered W2-1..W2-5 to avoid renumbering the lock.

### W2-1. Unplugged Hour, Discovery Park
- **Motif:** Phones face-down in a basket on a driftwood log; a group of friends watching orcas surface in the Sound.
- **Emotional beat:** Attention returned.
- **Composition:** Wide, figures backs to us, basket sharp foreground; Phthalo Turquoise water, Quinacridone Rose sunset.
- **Arc:** Wake — slot after #4.
- **Watercolor:** Orca fins as single Neutral Tint strokes on wet sea; basket dry-brush.

### W2-2. The Key on a String (edgier)
- **Motif:** A teenager in a Seattle bus shelter memorizing a handwritten list of words, a paper key on a string around her neck; ad screens behind her flicker unattended.
- **Emotional beat:** Defiant, quiet ownership — nobody holds this for her.
- **Composition:** Low angle, her face lit warm, screens as cold blurred rectangles; rain sheeting.
- **Arc:** Self-sovereignty — pairs with #8 Ledger.
- **Watercolor:** Screens wet-in-wet Neutral Tint/Ultramarine, deliberately unreadable; face glazed warm last. Edge stays hopeful, not paranoid.

### W2-3. Night Shift Garden, Cape Town
- **Motif:** A nurse after shift watering a rooftop vegetable plot at dawn, Lion's Head behind.
- **Emotional beat:** Self-reliance as care.
- **Composition:** 3/4 figure, hose arc catching light; saturated greens against violet-gold sky.
- **Arc:** Sovereignty → turning toward others; near #10.
- **Watercolor:** Water arc lifted out with a damp flat brush; masking for droplet sparkle.

### W2-4. Translator's Hands, Warsaw Tram
- **Motif:** Two strangers on a tram bridging a language gap with a sketchbook, drawing back and forth.
- **Emotional beat:** Delight of being understood.
- **Composition:** Close mid shot across the aisle; tram window streaking city lights.
- **Arc:** Choose People — between #12 and #13.
- **Watercolor:** Sketchbook drawings as tiny rigger lines; window streaks via tilted wet wash.

### W2-5. Lighthouse Handoff
- **Motif:** An old keeper handing a lantern to a young woman at the top of a PNW lighthouse stair, storm clearing outside.
- **Emotional beat:** Trust passed forward.
- **Composition:** Vertical, spiral stair, lantern the brightest point; window glowing clearing sky.
- **Arc:** Choose People — before #16 Builders.
- **Watercolor:** Wax-resist lantern glow; stair spiral in graded Ultramarine → Gamboge as light climbs. Distinct from #14 (no repair, no kids).
