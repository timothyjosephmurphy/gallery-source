# #9 Standing Wave — Easel Card — PRIVATE DRAFT · NOT on the paint lock (wave3 concept)
**Title / size / paper:** Half sheet (15×22 in) horizontal · 300 gsm cold press
**One-line beat:** A kayaker holds dead still in a standing wave while glacial white water churns around.
**Thumbnail:**
- Lights: masked spray, foam
- Mid: glacier turquoise water
- Darks: hull, paddle, figure — the only hard edges
**Palette:** Phthalo Green · Phthalo Turquoise · Ultramarine · Burnt Sienna (figure).
**Wash order:**
1. Pencil kayak and paddle line.
2. Masking fluid spatter for spray.
3. Wet-in-wet turquoise/green chaos, let it bloom.
4. Second chaotic layer, darker troughs.
5. Remove mask; soften a few spray edges.
6. Kayak and figure crisp, single pass.
**Keep white:** Spray and foam.
**TJ north star:** Calm agency inside chaos — steady enough now to help others.
**Don't:** No extreme-sports poster drama · don't harden the water.
**Done when:** Everything moves except the paddler.
**Rogan / interview Hook: "Self-sovereignty isn't escaping the rapids — it's learning to hold still in them."**
