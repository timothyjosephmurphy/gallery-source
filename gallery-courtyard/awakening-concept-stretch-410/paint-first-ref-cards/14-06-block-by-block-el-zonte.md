# #6 Block by Block (El Zonte) — Easel Card — PRIVATE DRAFT · NOT on the paint lock (wave3 concept)
**Title / size / paper:** Half sheet (15×22 in) vertical · 300 gsm cold press
**One-line beat:** A young surfer strides past a hand-painted wall of interlocking orange blocks, the Pacific blazing behind.
**Thumbnail:**
- Lights: surf foam, board edge, midday sky
- Mid: orange block wall, turquoise surf
- Darks: figure, mortar lines, cast shadow
**Palette:** Cadmium Orange · Phthalo Turquoise · Burnt Sienna · Ultramarine.
**Wash order:**
1. Pencil blocks as uneven hand-laid masonry; figure low-angle.
2. Reserve foam and the board.
3. Turquoise surf, hard horizon.
4. Blocks one by one, varying orange; dry between (patient effort).
5. Mortar lines Burnt Sienna + Ultramarine.
6. Figure warm skin, hard cast shadow.
**Keep white:** Board and foam crests.
**TJ north star:** Self-built confidence — Bitcoin only as subtle verified effort: blocks read as masonry, never a logo.
**Don't:** No ₿ symbol · no slogans · no postcard tourist clichés.
**Done when:** The wall reads as patient work stacked by hand and the surfer owns the frame.
**Rogan / interview Hook: "El Zonte is a small town that tried something new on its own terms — I'm painting the patience, not the price chart."**
