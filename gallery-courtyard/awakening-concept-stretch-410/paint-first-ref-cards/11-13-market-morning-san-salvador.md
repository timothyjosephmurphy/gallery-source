# #13 Market Morning, San Salvador — Easel Card — PRIVATE DRAFT (wave2 concept, NOT on paint lock)
**Title / size / paper:** Half sheet (15×22 in) horizontal eye-level · 300 gsm cold press
**One-line beat:** A fruit vendor laughing with a customer under a dappled tarp; mangoes and pitaya stacked in blazing color.
**Thumbnail:**
- Lights: dapple spots on faces and fruit tops
- Mid: tarp-filtered warm air, skin tones
- Darks: stall interior and shadow under the stacks
**Palette:** Cadmium Orange · Quinacridone Magenta (pitaya) · New Gamboge · Phthalo Turquoise (tarp).
**Wash order:**
1. Light pencil: two figures, stall, fruit masses (shapes, not every fruit).
2. Reserve dapple spots; turquoise tarp wash wet-in-wet.
3. Fruit masses: Gamboge→Orange mangoes, Magenta pitaya, charged wet.
4. Faces in warm skin glazes; laughter in the eyes/cheeks, not teeth detail.
5. Stall darks to push the fruit forward; final crisp dapples.
**Keep white:** Dapple spots + a highlight on the nearest mango.
**TJ north star:**
- Choosing people: ordinary exchange done in good faith.
- Everyday joy as trust — El Salvador travel language.
**Don't:** No identifiable real person · no poverty clichés · no political signage · no cartoon fruit.
**Done when:** The fruit is the loudest color and the laugh still reads at arm's length.
**Rogan / interview Hook: "What stuck with me in San Salvador wasn't policy — it was a vendor laughing with a regular. Trust is a daily trade between people."**
