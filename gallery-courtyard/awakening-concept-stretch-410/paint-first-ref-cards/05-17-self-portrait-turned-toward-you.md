# #17 Self-Portrait, Turned Toward You — Easel Card — PRIVATE DRAFT
**Title / size / paper:** Full sheet (22×30 in) vertical · 300 gsm cold press — finale
**One-line beat:** TJ half-turned from the easel, brush offered to the viewer; earlier paintings ghosted behind.
**Thumbnail:**
- Lights: window-lit face + brush tip + palette wells
- Mid: ghosted collage (#3 shards, #11 hands, lamps)
- Darks: easel edge, hair, one pass
**Palette:** Full spine palette at saturation; warm north window turned gold.
**Wash order:**
1. Pencil: 3/4 figure, foreshortened brush, easel.
2. Reserve face highlights + brush tip.
3. Ghost collage washes, pale, reinterpreted.
4. Face 2–3 confident layers.
5. Palette wells as jewel dots.
6. Darks one pass.
7. Final gold glaze on window side.
**Keep white:** Brush tip + cheek highlight.
**TJ north star:**
- Closes the series: "your turn."
- Collage recaps #3 → #11 → 11b → #12.
**Don't:** No cartoon proportions · no heroic poster pose · don't copy earlier pieces literally.
**Done when:** The offered brush is the first thing the viewer sees.
**Rogan / interview Hook: "I'm a watercolor painter in Seattle, not an expert — this series is my invitation to wake up hopeful and pick up your own brush."**
