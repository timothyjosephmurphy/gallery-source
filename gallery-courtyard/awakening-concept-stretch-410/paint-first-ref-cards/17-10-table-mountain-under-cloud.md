# #10 Table Mountain Under Cloud — Easel Card — PRIVATE DRAFT · NOT on the paint lock (wave3 concept)
**Title / size / paper:** Half sheet (15×22 in) vertical · 300 gsm cold press
**One-line beat:** A woman on a Cape Town rooftop at dusk; tablecloth cloud pours off the mountain; city light warms her face.
**Thumbnail:**
- Lights: cloud cascade, face highlight
- Mid: violet-to-gold sky, skin warm mids
- Darks: mountain mass, rooftop silhouette
**Palette:** Dioxazine Violet · Quinacridone Gold · Burnt Sienna · Neutral Tint.
**Wash order:**
1. Pencil low-angle 3/4 portrait and mountain line.
2. Reserve cloud tops and face highlight.
3. Violet-to-gold graded sky, wet.
4. Cloud cascade wet-in-wet, lift edges.
5. Mountain in Violet + Neutral Tint, hard top edge.
6. Face warm glazes; rooftop dark last.
**Keep white:** Cloud spilling over the edge.
**TJ north star:** Grounded strength — carrying history honestly, turning toward others.
**Don't:** No real person's likeness · no slogans · don't make the face pretty-generic.
**Done when:** Her face holds as warm as the sky, and the cloud feels like it's moving.
**Rogan / interview Hook: "South Africa's Truth and Reconciliation Commission (documented, 1996–98) is my touchstone — owning your story out loud is sovereignty too."**
