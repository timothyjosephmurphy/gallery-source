# #1 First Light Through Rain Glass — Easel Card — PRIVATE DRAFT (wave2 concept, NOT on paint lock)
**Title / size / paper:** Quarter sheet (11×15 in) vertical close 3/4 portrait · 300 gsm cold press
**One-line beat:** A face half-lit at a rain-streaked dawn window, eyes just opening; one cadmium-orange sunrise band cuts across the eyes.
**Thumbnail:**
- Lights: the warm band across the eyes — reserved paper, then one pale orange pass
- Mid: blue-gray city beyond, wet-in-wet rain streaks
- Darks: shadow side of the face and window frame, Ultramarine + Burnt Sienna
**Palette:** Cadmium Orange · Ultramarine · Phthalo Turquoise · Burnt Sienna.
**Wash order:**
1. Light pencil: window frame, 3/4 head, eye line, band edges.
2. Mask nothing — reserve the band by painting around it.
3. Wet-in-wet blue-gray city wash; drop vertical streaks while damp.
4. Dry fully; shadow side of face in Ultramarine/Sienna, soft into hair.
5. Single pale Cadmium Orange pass inside the band, hard edges top and bottom.
6. Darken window frame; lift two streaks back to light with a damp brush.
**Keep white:** Catchlights + the hottest strip of the band.
**TJ north star:**
- The moment of waking: "wait, I'm here."
- Same eye family as #3 and #12 — luminous, calm.
**Don't:** No eye-closed sleepy cliché · no cartoon rain drops · don't mud the band with blue · max 2 pigments per wash.
**Done when:** The warm stripe across the eyes is the first thing you see across the room.
**Rogan / interview Hook: "Waking up for me was small — noticing my own morning before the phone told me what to feel. That's the whole series in one window."**
