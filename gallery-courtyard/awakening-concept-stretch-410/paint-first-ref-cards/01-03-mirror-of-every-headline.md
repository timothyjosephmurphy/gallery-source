# #3 Mirror of Every Headline — Easel Card — PRIVATE DRAFT
**Title / size / paper:** Quarter sheet (11×15 in) vertical tight crop · 300 gsm cold press
**One-line beat:** Hands hold a cracked mirror; shards carry storm/screen/smoke, the reflected eye stays clear and calm.
**Thumbnail:**
- Lights: the reflected eye + one lifted catchlight
- Mid: shard glazes, cool rain-window side light
- Darks: Neutral Tint/Violet shard edges and fingers, hard-edged
**Palette:** Neutral Tint · Dioxazine Violet · Ultramarine · Phthalo Turquoise (iris only).
**Wash order:**
1. Light pencil: hands, mirror oval, shard lines, eye.
2. Mask/reserve eye whites and catchlight.
3. Pale Ultramarine side-light wash over hands.
4. Shards one at a time: storm/smoke wet-in-wet INSIDE each shard only.
5. Glaze shards 3–4 layers, dry between, hard edges.
6. Phthalo Turquoise iris, single clean pass.
7. Lift catchlight; soften hand edges.
**Keep white:** Eye whites + catchlight — brightest note on the sheet.
**TJ north star:**
- Opens the paint-first run: seeing the worst without flinching.
- The luminous eye returns in #12.
**Don't:** No logos or real faces · no paranoid iconography · don't mud the iris · max 2 pigments per wash.
**Done when:** The eye reads calm at arm's length while every shard reads stormy.
**Rogan / interview Hook: "Media literacy, not fear — the Iraq WMD coverage (documented, Senate Intel Committee 2004) taught me to look at headlines steadily, not to stop looking."**
