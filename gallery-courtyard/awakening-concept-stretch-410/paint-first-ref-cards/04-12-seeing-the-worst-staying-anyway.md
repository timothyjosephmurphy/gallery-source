# #12 Seeing the Worst, Staying Anyway — Easel Card — PRIVATE DRAFT
**Title / size / paper:** Half sheet (15×22 in) vertical · 300 gsm cold press
**One-line beat:** Two strangers under one umbrella at a bus stop after the storm; one has been crying, the other just stays.
**Thumbnail:**
- Lights: umbrella interior glow + tear line
- Mid: neon reflections red/teal on wet street
- Darks: figures and shelter, Neutral Tint street
**Palette:** Quin Rose + Phthalo Turquoise (neon) · Cadmium Orange (umbrella) · Neutral Tint (street).
**Wash order:**
1. Pencil: umbrella dome, two figures, shelter.
2. Reserve umbrella interior and tear.
3. Street: tilt board, pull reflections down wet-in-wet.
4. Dry. Figures one pass.
5. Umbrella glazed warm LAST.
6. Lift single tear line.
7. Soften reflection edges.
**Keep white:** Umbrella interior; tear line lifted.
**TJ north star:**
- Central thesis: see the worst, choose people.
- Luminous eye from #3 returns, now looking at someone.
**Don't:** No melodrama · no overworked faces · neon must not out-glow umbrella.
**Done when:** The warm dome reads as shelter from across the room.
**Rogan / interview Hook: "Knowing the documented stuff — Tuskegee, MKUltra's declassified files — made me want to be the stranger who stays, not the one who leaves."**
