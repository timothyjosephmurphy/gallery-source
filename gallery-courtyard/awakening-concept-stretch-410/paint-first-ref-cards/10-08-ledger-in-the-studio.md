# #8 The Ledger in the Studio — Easel Card — PRIVATE DRAFT (wave2 concept, NOT on paint lock)
**Title / size / paper:** Quarter sheet (11×15 in) horizontal overhead · 300 gsm cold press
**One-line beat:** A watercolor palette beside a small notebook of hand-tallied days, every square checked, one brush mid-stroke.
**Thumbnail:**
- Lights: notebook page + the brush tip's wet sheen
- Mid: warm lamp pool vs cool window side
- Darks: table edges, palette rim, under-notebook shadow
**Palette:** Quinacridone Gold · Phthalo Turquoise · Quinacridone Rose · Ultramarine (palette wells = jewel dots).
**Wash order:**
1. Light pencil: notebook grid, palette wells, brush diagonal.
2. Warm lamp wash (Q. Gold) right, cool Ultramarine left, meet wet.
3. Dry; tally ticks in small dark strokes — irregular, hand-made.
4. Palette wells as pure saturated dots, one pigment each.
5. Shadows under notebook and brush; one lift on the brush ferrule.
**Keep white:** Notebook page (ticks only) + brush highlight.
**TJ north star:**
- Pride in proof: work verified by doing it daily.
- Bitcoin only as metaphor for patient, verifiable effort — no logo, no coin.
**Don't:** No ₿ symbol, no charts or prices · don't grid the page with a ruler · max 2 pigments per wash.
**Done when:** The jewel wells pop and the ledger reads as hand-kept days, not a spreadsheet.
**Rogan / interview Hook: "Sound money taught me a studio habit: proof of work is just showing up daily and keeping the tally honest — a metaphor, not a sales pitch."**
