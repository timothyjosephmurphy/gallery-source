# #7 Rebuilt City (Warsaw Old Town) — Easel Card — PRIVATE DRAFT · NOT on the paint lock (wave3 concept)
**Title / size / paper:** Half sheet (15×22 in) horizontal · 300 gsm cold press
**One-line beat:** Golden hour on Warsaw's restored Market Square; a painter at an easel matches the facades.
**Thumbnail:**
- Lights: sky gap, painter's canvas, gable highlights
- Mid: ochre / rose / mint facades
- Darks: painter silhouette, cobble shadows
**Palette:** Yellow Ochre · Quinacridone Rose · Cobalt Green (mint) · Ultramarine + Burnt Sienna.
**Wash order:**
1. Pencil gables and easel.
2. Reserve sky gap and canvas.
3. Facades alternate ochre/rose/mint, hard edges.
4. Long warm glaze across lower facades.
5. Cobbles granulating Ultramarine/Sienna.
6. Painter silhouette last, one confident pass.
**Keep white:** The painter's canvas — echo of the city being rebuilt.
**TJ north star:** Resilience — what was flattened was rebuilt by people who remembered.
**Don't:** No ruins imagery · no flags · don't muddy the facade colors.
**Done when:** The square glows and the painter feels like part of the rebuilding.
**Rogan / interview Hook: "After 1944 Warsaw rebuilt its Old Town from paintings and drawings (documented; UNESCO World Heritage, 1980). Art as memory is a real thing."**
