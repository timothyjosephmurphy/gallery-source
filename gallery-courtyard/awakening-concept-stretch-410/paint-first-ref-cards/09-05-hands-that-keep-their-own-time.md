# #5 Hands That Keep Their Own Time — Easel Card — PRIVATE DRAFT (wave2 concept, NOT on paint lock)
**Title / size / paper:** Quarter sheet (11×15 in) horizontal overhead flat-lay · 300 gsm cold press
**One-line beat:** Weathered hands wind a small mechanical clock on a workbench; one tiny orange gleam on a gear.
**Thumbnail:**
- Lights: clock face + knuckle highlights from left window
- Mid: workbench wood, warm Burnt Sienna glaze
- Darks: long raking shadows in Ultramarine/Sienna, gear interior
**Palette:** Burnt Sienna · Ultramarine · Raw Sienna · Cadmium Orange (gear gleam only).
**Wash order:**
1. Light pencil: clock circle, hands, gear, shadow directions (all to the right).
2. Raw Sienna bench wash, wet-in-wet grain.
3. Dry; hands in warm flesh glaze, cool in the creases.
4. Long shadows in one pass, Ultramarine + Sienna, hard edge.
5. Gear darks; then one dot of Cadmium Orange on a tooth.
**Keep white:** Clock face + two knuckle highlights.
**TJ north star:**
- Sovereignty starts with time and attention.
- Quiet, patient-effort thread — no slogans.
**Don't:** No brand on the clock · no readable numerals needed · don't let shadows go purple-mud.
**Done when:** The gear gleam is the only saturated warm and pulls the eye straight to it.
**Rogan / interview Hook: "Owning my hours is the first freedom I can actually practice — the clock is mine because I wind it."**
