# #16 The Builders at Dawn — Easel Card — PRIVATE DRAFT (wave2 concept, NOT on paint lock)
**Title / size / paper:** Half sheet (15×22 in) wide, slightly low angle · 300 gsm cold press
**One-line beat:** A diverse crew raises a community greenhouse frame at sunrise; a softly glowing helper drone holds a beam steady.
**Thumbnail:**
- Lights: sunrise behind the frame, drone glow
- Mid: greens and golds of ground and figures
- Darks: ribbed frame silhouettes and long shadows toward the viewer
**Palette:** New Gamboge · Cadmium Orange · Sap/Phthalo Green · Ultramarine (shadows).
**Wash order:**
1. Light pencil: horizon, greenhouse ribs, 4–5 figures, drone, beam.
2. Sky: Gamboge→Orange glow centered behind frame, wet-in-wet.
3. Dry; ground in saturated greens, gold rim on top.
4. Ribs and figures as warm-dark silhouettes, hard edges.
5. Long ribbed shadows in Ultramarine, one pass.
6. Drone: reserved white with a soft turquoise halo — gentle, not menacing.
**Keep white:** Sun core + drone glow.
**TJ north star:**
- Humans and intelligence choosing a shared future.
- Collaborative hope; Super intelligent Beauty language.
**Don't:** No sci-fi chrome · no menacing robot · figures not posed like a stock ad · no logos.
**Done when:** The ribbed shadows lead the eye to the people, and the drone reads as a helper.
**Rogan / interview Hook: "Waking up doesn't mean fearing the machines — it means choosing who we build with. I paint the version where we raise the greenhouse together."**
