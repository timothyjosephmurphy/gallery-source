# #14 Elders Teaching the Kids to Fix Things — Easel Card — PRIVATE DRAFT · NOT on the paint lock (wave3 concept)
**Title / size / paper:** Half sheet (15×22 in) horizontal · 300 gsm cold press
**One-line beat:** An older man and two kids fix a bicycle in a Seattle alley, golden backlight.
**Thumbnail:**
- Lights: backlit alley gap, rim light on hair, lifted spokes
- Mid: warm skin, brick
- Darks: tires, alley shadows
**Palette:** Quinacridone Gold · Cadmium Orange · Burnt Sienna · Ultramarine.
**Wash order:**
1. Pencil from kid height; wheels as ellipses.
2. Reserve alley light and rim lights.
3. Warm gold glow through the gap.
4. Figures Sienna/Ultramarine, soft-edged.
5. Tires dark, hard.
6. Lift spokes with damp flat brush; final rim light check.
**Keep white:** Rim light on hair + the alley gap.
**TJ north star:** Inheritance — skill passed hand to hand; choosing people across generations.
**Don't:** No sentimental clichés · no brand names on the bike.
**Done when:** Hands and spokes read clearly and the light feels like a passing-on.
**Rogan / interview Hook: "Real independence is knowing how to fix your own stuff — and teaching a kid to."**
