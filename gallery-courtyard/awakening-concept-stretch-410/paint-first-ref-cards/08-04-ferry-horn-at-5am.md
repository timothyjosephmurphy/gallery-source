# #4 Ferry Horn at 5 A.M. — Easel Card — PRIVATE DRAFT (wave2 concept, NOT on paint lock)
**Title / size / paper:** Half sheet (15×22 in) wide horizontal · 300 gsm cold press
**One-line beat:** A lone passenger at a Washington State ferry bow as fog peels back to a burning-pink Mount Rainier.
**Thumbnail:**
- Lights: fog breaking near the horizon, wake foam
- Mid: graded fog, Rainier in rose/violet
- Darks: bow rail and the small figure, lower third
**Palette:** Quinacridone Rose · Dioxazine Violet · Ultramarine · Neutral Tint (figure/rail only).
**Wash order:**
1. Light pencil: horizon, mountain silhouette, bow lines, figure.
2. Graded fog wash top→bottom, Ultramarine fading to paper at horizon.
3. Dry; water band in cool gray-blue, leave wake white.
4. Mountain dropped in LAST: Rose on peak, Violet on shadow flank, wet edge into fog.
5. Bow and rail Neutral Tint, hard-edged; figure small, one dark shape.
**Keep white:** Wake foam + the fog gap under the peak.
**TJ north star:**
- Waking becomes wonder, not fear.
- PNW scale: the world was this big all along.
**Don't:** No ferry logos or livery · figure not centered · don't overwork the mountain (one pass + one glaze max).
**Done when:** The pink peak glows against the fog and the figure feels tiny but steady.
**Rogan / interview Hook: "Every Seattle commuter knows the 5 a.m. ferry. Awe is the antidote to doom-scrolling — you can't feel small and scared at the same time when Rainier comes out."**
