# #15 Long Table, Many Languages — Easel Card — PRIVATE DRAFT
*Agent-proposed concept card — NOT on the paint lock (#3 → #11 → 11b → #12 → #17; 5b = BRIDGE only). Paint decisions stay with TJ in the morning.*
**Title / size / paper:** Half sheet (15×22 in) horizontal · 300 gsm cold press
**One-line beat:** Belonging — a long outdoor table at dusk runs to one vanishing point under string lights; many faces, one meal.
**Thumbnail:**
- Lights: warm tablecloth wedge + string-light bulbs along the two converging wires
- Mid: loose face washes down both sides, dishes as warm dabs
- Darks: deep Ultramarine/Indanthrone dusk sky and ground at the edges
**Palette:** Ultramarine · Indanthrone Blue · Quinacridone Gold · Burnt Sienna · Permanent Rose (cheeks/dishes only).
**Wash order:**
1. Light pencil: vanishing point, table edges, two light wires, head ovals shrinking with distance.
2. Mask/reserve bulbs and the near tablecloth highlight.
3. Graded dusk sky, Indanthrone top → Ultramarine at the horizon, one pass.
4. Quinacridone Gold tablecloth wedge, warm to pale toward the vanishing point.
5. Faces wet-in-wet, loose: Burnt Sienna + Rose, varied tones, few details; near faces largest.
6. Dishes: small dabs hinting at Warsaw pierogi, Cape Town bobotie, Seattle salmon.
7. Remove mask; soft gold halo glaze around each bulb; darken ground edges.
**Keep white:** Bulbs + nearest plate rim.
**TJ north star:**
- Choosing people at scale: #12's two strangers become a table of many.
- Warsaw/Cape Town/Seattle echo #7 and #10 in faces and dishes.
**Don't:** No real or recognizable faces · no flags or slogans · don't tighten the faces · max 2 pigments per wash.
**Done when:** At arm's length the eye runs down the table to the light, and every seat reads as welcome.
**Rogan / interview Hook (pitch prep only, no interview booked): "You don't need a shared language to share a table. Sitting down with people you didn't grow up with is a choice you make. Choosing people one plate at a time is how belonging scales."**
