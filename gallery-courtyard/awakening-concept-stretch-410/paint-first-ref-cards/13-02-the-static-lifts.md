# #2 The Static Lifts — Easel Card — PRIVATE DRAFT · NOT on the paint lock (wave3 concept)
**Title / size / paper:** Half sheet (15×22 in) vertical · 300 gsm cold press
**One-line beat:** From above, a gray crosswalk crowd; one figure in full color looks up.
**Thumbnail:**
- Lights: crosswalk stripes + the lifted face
- Mid: granulating crowd, wet asphalt
- Darks: figure shadows, Neutral Tint pooling
**Palette:** Neutral Tint · Ultramarine · Quinacridone Magenta · Phthalo Turquoise (central figure only).
**Wash order:**
1. Pencil stripes and figure ovals from high angle.
2. Reserve stripes and the upturned face.
3. Ultramarine + Neutral Tint granulating wash over street and crowd.
4. Crowd shadows wet-on-dry, soft edges.
5. Central figure: Magenta coat, Turquoise scarf, clean hard edges.
6. Final darks under feet; soften crowd edges further.
**Keep white:** Stripes and the face catching sky light.
**TJ north star:** Separation without contempt — the crowd is painted tenderly, just still asleep.
**Don't:** No sneering faces · no cartoon contrast · don't let color leak into the crowd.
**Done when:** The eye lands on the one color figure in under a second, and the crowd still feels kind.
**Rogan / interview Hook: "Waking up isn't feeling superior — it's noticing color you'd stopped seeing, then going back for your neighbors."**
