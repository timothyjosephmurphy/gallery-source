# Awakening — Suggested Progression (#410) — PRIVATE

**Arc:** wake → self-sovereignty → choose people. 17 pieces, three movements.

## Movement I — WAKE (1–4)
Cool, rain-gray grounds with one saturated intrusion of color per piece. Open intimate (1), widen to the crowd (2), confront the hard stuff (3), resolve in awe (4).
Order: 1 First Light → 2 Static Lifts → 3 Mirror of Every Headline → 4 Ferry Horn.

## Movement II — SELF-SOVEREIGNTY (5–11)
Warmer palette, harder edges, travel enters. Personal (5, 8) alternates with place (6, 7, 10) so it doesn't turn into a travelogue. 9 is the steadiness peak; 11 is the hinge, the edgy piece that turns sovereignty outward toward collaboration.
Order: 5 Hands → 6 El Zonte → 7 Warsaw → 8 Ledger → 9 Standing Wave → 10 Table Mountain → 11 Machine Offers Its Hand.

## Movement III — CHOOSE PEOPLE (12–17)
Full saturation, warm light, more figures per frame. Two strangers (12) → a pair (13) → generations (14) → a crowd (15) → a future-building crew (16) → the viewer (17).
Order: 12 Umbrella → 13 Market → 14 Bike Repair → 15 Long Table → 16 Builders → 17 Self-Portrait.

## Anchor pieces (paint first if time is short)
3 (edge), 11 (hinge / most noticeable), 12 (thesis), 17 (close). These four tell the whole arc alone.

## Palette drift
Neutral tint + one accent → ochres/turquoise/hard edges → full-spectrum warm glow. Test every piece under gray north light; if it goes dull, push saturation one step.
