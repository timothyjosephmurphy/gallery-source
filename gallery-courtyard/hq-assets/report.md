**Exterior (`exterior/`, tileable PBR, desktop/phone):** `ext-stucco` (near-neutral light facade stucco; the code multiplies it by the six sunrise facade colours, see `preview-exterior.png` 'x tint'), `ext-travertine` (plinth, jambs), `ext-paving` (path lanes), `ext-gravel` and `ext-lawn` (ground), `ext-foliage` (boxwood/cypress/laurel), `ext-roof-tile` (no roof exists in code today: optional new surface), `ext-cornice-clay` (terracotta cornice), `ext-trim-slate` (door surrounds), `ext-ivy-card` (RGBA leaf card 4:3 for the 0.16x0.12 m ivy planes). Surfaces and roles were taken from play-tags `courtyardstyle.js`, `tags.js` and gallery-core `garden/garden.js` (read-only). `_n` maps are included where relief reads (not for lawn/roof roughness); sizes: 2048/1024 for facade, paving, gravel, lawn, 1024/512 for the rest. Colour q88 (gravel, lawn, foliage q72).
- Roles with vertex colours (hedges, ivy, lawn panels) multiply the albedo, so set instance/vertex colours to white or ~0.85 or these greens will go dark. Facade skins are the opposite on purpose: keep their colour.
- Not covered: cypress and laurel are meant to share `ext-foliage`; fountain water, glass domes (already exist) and marble benches (reuse `prop-marble`) have no new art.

**Signs (`signs/`):** text-free plates `sign-plate-enamel`, `sign-plate-brass`, `sign-plate-wood`, 1024x256 / 512x128 (4:1) RGBA with `_n`/`_r`; lettering stays in code (`labelTex`/canvas). The code's signs are two kinds: door/nave labels (flat planes, ~0.22 aspect, gates-sign) and the floor mosaic arc bands from `signs.json` (24 deg at 0.8 apothem, band 0.12). Plates are symmetric and un-perspectived, so they bend onto the arc by mapping u along arc length and v across the band (`preview-signs.png` shows a plate on a 24 deg arc); the existing arc signs are baked mosaic with text and are not replaced. Lettering area `text_rect_uv` = [0.07, 0.20, 0.93, 0.80] (in manifest). Brass has `metalness` 1.0. Demo text in the preview is not part of the assets. Note: the arc band geometry works out to about 2.9:1 while the current sign PNGs are 3.9:1 (1024x262), so those existing textures are probably stretched on the arc; the plates are 4:1, so crop or scale v if you use them there.

**Baked AO (`ao/`):** (a) `ao-plan-floor` (2048 / 1024) and `ao-plan-ceiling` (1024 / 512) are real top-down lightmaps baked from the live layout (a read-only `makeWorld(layout.tags)` dump: 112 walls, 13 enclosed rooms, door gaps respected). Sample by world x,z: u=(x-x0)/size, v=(z-z0)/size with x0=-42, z0=-29, size=72 m (`ao/ao-plan.json`); multiply into floor/ground and ceiling. Rooms get stronger contact + broad occlusion, open courts contact only, the central hall is treated as open. (b) Vertex-colour AO was **not** done: wall/floor meshes are built in code at runtime and Gallery's code is off limits, so instead there are RGBA black decal strips (alpha = occlusion): `ao-wall-base` (0.8 m), `ao-wall-ceiling` (0.6 m), `ao-corner` (0.8 x 4.2 m), `ao-edge` (door jambs) and `ao-contact-blob`, with exact placement data in `ao/ao-placement.json` (78 room corners, 112 wall segments with normals, 30 doors) and usage notes per file in the manifest. Use `transparent`, `depthWrite:false`, a small `polygonOffset`, 3 cm off the face. Previews: `preview-ao.png`.
- Limits: AO assumes 4.2 m walls and 0.16 m wall thickness from the layout; benches, pillars, the fountain and art frames are not baked (use the contact blob). It was not tested in the engine.

## Seamless tiling check

Method: (1) every tileable is built from periodic ingredients (FFT noise, wrapped Voronoi, integer-cycle patterns, wrap-padded resizes for the phone tier); (2) numeric test on the encoded pixels: step across the wrap-around edge vs the largest ordinary neighbouring-pixel step in the same image, worst axis (pass = wrap step ≤ 1.05 × max interior step; for decoded WebP, also allowed +2 levels of codec edge error); (3) a 3x3 tile render for each (`previews/tile3x3/`).

Result: raw pixels, all pass; decoded WebP files, all pass (46 colour files checked incl. phone tier; sky checked horizontally only). Numbers in `previews/seam-scores.json` [wrap step, mean interior step, max interior step] in 0-255 levels. One real seam was found and fixed during QA (prop-marble vein term had a non-integer vertical period). Normal/roughness maps are derived from the same periodic height fields with wrapped gradients, so they tile too (not separately render-tested). Visible repetition: plaster, ashlar and flagstone show a faint repeat at 3x3 (single-tile scale), so use larger repeat sizes or a macro-variation overlay.

## Integration notes for Gallery (not applied)

- Roles map 1:1 to `play-tags` `mo.stone/floor/ceiling/metal` (`wall_*`, `floor_*`, `metal_*` in `public/pbr`). Existing ARM maps pack AO/rough/metal into R/G/B; the new `_r` files are plain roughness, so set `roughnessMap` = `_r`, drop `aoMap`/`metalnessMap`, and set `metalness` as a constant (1.0 for prop-brass, 0 otherwise) and `roughness: 1`.
- The current in-game tints (wall 0xB8ADA0, floor 0x6E665E, ceiling 0x6A5850) are multiplied in and darken everything; the new colours are already graded to the palette, so use white (or a light tint) or they will look muddy.
- Current phone path loads `*_diff_1k` for walls/floor only; new `.phone.webp` names follow the brief. Normal maps are half the size of colour on walls/floors (1024 desktop / 512 phone), which is plenty for the relief amplitude.
- Sky: the scene currently has no sky texture (flat 0xCFE0EA background, 0xD8C4A4 env colour + fog). Use `sky-pnw-day.webp` as an equirectangular `scene.background` (colorSpace sRGB, mapping EquirectangularReflectionMapping) and optionally PMREM it for `scene.environment`. Its lower half is water to the horizon; the sun is at ~ azimuth 126°, 29° elevation, the volcano peak at azimuth ~286°.
- Cloud layers/ridges are baked, so sky is static; the horizon-haze colour is warm Dawn Plaster/Apricot to sit with the fog colours.

## Not covered / limits

- Scope was textures for walls, ceilings, floors, sky and interior-prop materials. Not done: courtyard exterior, fountain, signs, frames geometry, HUD tool models, paintings, LOD/baked AO, GLBs (those belong to Gallery or a separate pass).
- Not tested in the live three.js scene (Gallery's repos were off limits and no preview build was made); judged from 2D previews and a numpy-lit check only. Terrazzo, flagstone and plaster may need exposure tweaks in-engine.
- Procedural, not photographic: the plaster/felt/marble are deliberately calm so they do not compete with the paintings.
