# Paint-First — Watercolor Execution Notes (#468 wave2) — PRIVATE
Deepens wave1 paint-first picks #1, #3, #11, #13. Painting subjects only — not storyboards.
**Shared base:** Arches 300 lb cold press, stretched or blocked; full sheet trimmed to half (15×22) unless noted. Bright, saturated, PNW-light-friendly. Watercolor + watercolor pastel accents on dry passes only.
**Shared palette (pick per piece):** Quinacridone Rose, New Gamboge, Cobalt Blue, Ultramarine (French), Phthalo Turquoise, Burnt Sienna, Quinacridone Gold, Dioxazine Violet, Raw Sienna.
**Never:** crypto chrome, coin logos, glowing orange ₿, neon circuitry, cartoon faces/big eyes, outlined comic edges, muddy overworked greys.

---

## #1 Two Notebooks, One Kitchen Table
- **Paper:** Arches 140 lb CP quarter sheet works too — intimacy favors small.
- **Palette:** Raw Sienna + Quinacridone Gold (table), Cobalt Blue (shadows), Quinacridone Rose (warm skin), Burnt Sienna (coffee rings), Ultramarine (ink lines).
- **Light:** Kitchen morning, low raking from left; long cobalt shadows of the pen and cup.
- **Technique:** Reserve page whites (no masking—paint around). Coffee rings: drop diluted Burnt Sienna into a damp circle, let edges bloom and dry hard — the overlapping ring is the "oh, you too" moment. Handwriting suggested with rigger, never legible.
- **Wax resist:** Optional — a single clear-wax tick in each notebook margin as the faint "first block" mark, invisible until the wash goes over.
- **Brushwork:** Loose wet-in-wet table grain first, then crisp dry-brush on knuckles and page edges.
- **Don't:** Show faces, show any screen, make the ink glow.

## #3 The Generals at Low Tide
- **Paper:** Arches 300 lb CP, panoramic strip (10×30) for the shoreline line.
- **Palette:** Dioxazine Violet + Ultramarine (dusk sky), Phthalo Turquoise (tide pools), New Gamboge + Quinacridone Rose (lanterns), Burnt Sienna (rocks), one lantern in Phthalo Turquoise-tinged green (the false light).
- **Light:** El Zonte dusk, sun just under the horizon; lanterns are the only warm notes.
- **Technique:** Wax-resist dots before the first sky wash for each lantern halo — the "genesis" resist; drop Gamboge into the resisted dots after. Graded wet-in-wet sky, then rocks charged in wet so they sit low and dark. Reflections: vertical lifted streaks with a thirsty brush.
- **Brushwork:** Figures as 3–4 strokes each; no detail. The off-color lantern must be visibly *painted with care*, not a mistake.
- **Don't:** Draw lines connecting lanterns (no network diagram), no overt symbols on the lanterns.

## #11 The Pages Begin to Fuse
- **Paper:** Arches 140 lb CP, small (9×12). Fast piece — one sitting.
- **Palette:** Ultramarine + Burnt Sienna (Seattle grey, mixed on paper not palette), Quinacridone Gold (edge light), Indigo-leaning Ultramarine ink, Quinacridone Rose (one warm bleed).
- **Light:** Rainy windowsill, grey turning gold at right edge.
- **Technique:** The process is the subject: paint both notebooks' "text" as damp ink lines, then spritz/drag a wet brush across the gutter so they bloom together. Accept the backrun — it's the fusion. Window rain as salt or lifted streaks on the glass only.
- **Brushwork:** Minimal; let water do it. Stop early.
- **Don't:** Rescue the blooms, add legible words, add a hard outline around the sill.

## #13 Nine Pages at Dawn
- **Paper:** Arches 300 lb CP, half sheet vertical; the paper's untouched white *is* the white paper.
- **Palette:** New Gamboge + Quinacridone Rose (dawn wall glow), Cobalt Blue (cool shadows under cups), Raw Sienna (chairs), Phthalo Turquoise (window frame / sky), Burnt Sienna (wood).
- **Light:** Dawn, window behind-left, strong; the page sits in full light.
- **Technique:** Never touch the paper rectangle — not even a stroke. Optional clear-wax resist of nine faint parallel lines across it (the nine pages; readable only at raking angle). Saturate everything around it to push the white forward. Cups still faintly steaming: lifted soft wisps.
- **Brushwork:** Big confident washes on the wall, controlled edges on chair backs (just pulled out — angle them).
- **Don't:** Title on the page, any symbol, figures; don't grey down the room.
