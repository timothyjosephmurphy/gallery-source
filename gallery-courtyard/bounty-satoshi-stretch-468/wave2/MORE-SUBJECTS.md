## 15. Salmon Run Consensus
- **Motif:** Salmon in a Cascade creek all turning upstream together; one fish drifts the wrong way and the run simply flows past it.
- **Emotional beat:** Relief — the honest majority doesn't need to chase the liar.
- **Composition:** Overhead through clear water, river stones in Burnt Sienna/Raw Sienna, fish as Quinacridone Rose + Phthalo Turquoise strokes all angled one way; the stray one quietly against grain. Tanaka's boots at the bank edge, cropped.
- **Extends:** PNW rivers; nature metaphors.
- **Act II beat:** Byzantine fault tolerance as current — consensus routes around faults.
- **Watercolor notes:** Wet-in-wet water, fish dropped in on damp then sharpened with one dry stroke each; light dapple via lifted spots.

## 16. Heron Who Says It in One Line
- **Motif:** Esperanza watching a great blue heron stand motionless on a single reed stalk at slack tide; her notebook page holds one brushed line.
- **Emotional beat:** Delight at elegance.
- **Composition:** Horizontal, heron silhouette mid-right, figure small left; pearly fog, one Cobalt/Ultramarine stroke for the heron's neck.
- **Extends:** Puget Sound marsh; companion to #5 without repeating its wave.
- **Act II beat:** Kolmogorov complexity — the shortest true description is a living thing.
- **Watercolor notes:** Fog via graded wash on rewetted paper; heron painted in one breath with a large round, no corrections.

## 17. Snowpack Ledger
- **Motif:** A mountain hut wall in the Olympics with snow-depth tally marks painted by years of hikers; the couple add today's mark.

## 19. Two Rivers Meeting, Hood Canal
- **Motif:** A clear freshwater stream entering green saltwater, the seam visible; on a log, both notebooks open, one weighted by a stone.

## 20. Lantern Kept Through the Storm

- **Composition:** Interior, warm lamp core vs Dioxazine/Ultramarine storm window; log book centered.
- **Extends:** Coastal Washington; echoes #9 lanterns as a single steady one.
- **Act II beat:** Liveness under attack — the record keeps going.
- **Watercolor notes:** Wax-resist the lamp glow ring; storm via wet-in-wet with tilted board; lamp light in New Gamboge glazed last.
