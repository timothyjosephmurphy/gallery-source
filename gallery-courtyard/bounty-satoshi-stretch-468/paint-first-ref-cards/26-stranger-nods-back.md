# #26 A Stranger Nods Back — Easel Card — PRIVATE
**Title / size / paper:** A Stranger Nods Back · 11×15 in landscape · 300 gsm cold press
**One-line beat:** Quiet joy across a snowy square — a stranger confirms the work; the network begins.
**Thumbnail:**
- Lights: two windows (left café, right far window) + final snow dots
- Mid: snowy square, tram lines, sky gradient
- Darks: building masses framing windows, one-stroke figure silhouettes
**Palette:** Ultramarine (night) · Quin Rose (violet tint into Ultramarine) · Indian Yellow (window core) · Quin Gold (window base) · tone Burnt Sienna (darks w/ Ultramarine). White gouache = snow only.
**Wash order:**
1. Light pencil: two windows, tram lines converging between them.
2. Graded sky→square Ultramarine + touch Rose, wet-in-wet, around dry window shapes.
3. Dry fully. Second glaze on building masses only.
4. Windows: Indian Yellow, drop Quin Gold into lower edge.
5. Figures: one stroke each, Ultramarine + Burnt Sienna; Tanaka's head tilted up, stranger mid-nod.
6. Tram lines with rigger; lift 2–3 glints.
7. Bone dry → gouache snow spatter.
**Keep white:** Both window shapes (dry hard edge) until step 4; a sliver of glint on tram rails.
**TJ north star:**
- Warsaw night: blue-violet depth, warm windows singing.
- BoS portraits: tiny figures still read as people — posture tells the nod.
**Don't:** No more than 2 pigments per wash (mud) · no big figures — space is the story · no chain/lantern/crypto chrome.
**Done when:** The two windows glow as a pair and the nod reads from across the room.
