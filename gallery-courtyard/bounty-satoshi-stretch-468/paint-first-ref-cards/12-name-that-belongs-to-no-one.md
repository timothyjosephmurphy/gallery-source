# #12 A Name That Belongs to No One — Easel Card — PRIVATE
**Title / size / paper:** A Name That Belongs to No One · 11×15 in portrait · 300 gsm cold press
**One-line beat:** Selfless humility — marks in wet sand half-erased by foam, footprints leading away.
**Thumbnail:**
- Lights: foam lace edge, wet-sand sheen
- Mid: sand, pastel sunrise reflection
- Darks: carved mark shadows, footprint hollows
**Palette:** Quin Rose (pastel sunrise) · Quin Gold · Cobalt Blue (water, wet sand) · Raw Sienna (sand) · Burnt Sienna (shadows w/ Cobalt).
**Wash order:**
1. Light pencil: high angle, foam diagonal top, abstract marks centre, footprint trail to edge.
2. Wet-in-wet sand: Raw Sienna, drop Rose + Gold for sunrise reflection.
3. Water top: Cobalt, leave foam edge white (masking optional).
4. Dry. Carved marks as abstract soft strokes (no legible letters), Burnt Sienna + Cobalt.
5. Footprints: small hollows, soft lower edges, fading in value toward frame edge.
6. Lift foam into marks to show erasure.
**Keep white:** Foam lace; sheen streak on wet sand.
**TJ north star:**
- El Zonte shoreline: pastel morning light.
- Berlín mural sensibility: marks as gesture, not lettering.
**Don't:** No readable name/letters · no figures (footprints only) · no hard outlines.
**Done when:** The marks feel like they're disappearing and the footprints pull the eye off-frame.
