# #34 Fingerprint of a Leaf — Easel Card — PRIVATE
**Title / size / paper:** Fingerprint of a Leaf · 11×15 in portrait · 300 gsm cold press
**One-line beat:** Wonder in one honest mark — a palm presses a maple leaf and the print is unique and unforgeable.
**Thumbnail:**
- Lights: reserved vein network + dapple spots on hand and paper
- Mid: leaf greens, warm skin, emerging print
- Darks: cool shadow under fingers, print's darkest dry-brush tooth, knuckle creases
**Palette:** Indian Yellow (leaf core) · Sap Green (leaf body) · Quin Rose (stem, skin warmth) · Phthalo Blue GS (finger shadow, dapple glaze) · tone Burnt Sienna (print, knuckle shadows).
**Wash order:**
1. Light pencil: hand, leaf, print outline below — tight overhead, fill the frame.
2. Reserve vein network and dapple spots (dry paper edges).
3. Leaf wet-in-wet Indian Yellow → Sap Green; touch Rose at stem.
4. Dry fully. Lift veins with a damp flat.
5. Skin: Rose + Indian Yellow; cool Phthalo shadow under fingers.
6. Print: dry-brush Burnt Sienna across the rough tooth.
7. Dapple: pale Phthalo glaze around reserved lights.
**Keep white:** Dapple spots until step 7; vein highlights (lift if lost).
**TJ north star:**
- Saturated, compact study — leaf glows, skin glows.
- The print reads as a fingerprint of the whole leaf: small, exact, honest.
**Don't:** No more than 2 pigments per wash (olive mud) · no over-literal veins · no stiff hand · no hex/code overlays.
**Done when:** The leaf glows green-gold and the print reads as one-of-a-kind from arm's length.
