# #23 Messengers Over the Pass — Easel Card — PRIVATE
**Title / size / paper:** Messengers Over the Pass · 9×20 in panoramic · 300 gsm cold press
**One-line beat:** Steadiness — trust built from agreement across distance.
**Thumbnail:**
- Lights: Four fire reserves + dusk sky edge
- Mid: Layered ridges cool→warm
- Darks: Nearest ridge, riders (3 strokes each)
**Palette:** Cobalt/Ultramarine (far ridges) · Burnt Sienna (near ridge) · Cadmium Orange (3 fires) · Quin Magenta (odd fire). White gouache sparingly only.
**Wash order:**
1. Light pencil: 4 ridge lines, fire dots, 3 rider positions.
2. Sky graded dusk, around fire dots.
3. Ridges as successive flat glazes, lightest first, dry between.
4. Fires: drop Cad Orange into 3 reserves; Quin Magenta into far odd one.
5. Riders: three strokes each, on different ridges.
6. Soft glow lift around agreeing fires.
7. Stop — don't overwork.
**Keep white:** Fire dots until step 4.
**TJ north star:**
- Generals (#3) moved inland.
- BoS portraits: tiny figures still read as people — posture tells the story.
**Don't:** No coast/sea · no flags/text · no detailed horses. · no more than 2 pigments per wash.
**Done when:** Three fires agree, the odd one is seen and ignored.
