# #17 Snowpack Ledger — Easel Card — PRIVATE
**Title / size / paper:** Snowpack Ledger · 11×15 in landscape · 300 gsm cold press
**One-line beat:** Humility — joining a record kept by strangers; append-only history.
**Thumbnail:**
- Lights: Doorway snowfield + wax-resist tally marks
- Mid: Weathered cedar boards, warm grey
- Darks: Door frame, board seams, two figure shapes at wall
**Palette:** Cerulean/Ultramarine (snowfield, saturated) · Burnt Sienna + Quin Gold (cedar) · Ultramarine + Burnt Sienna (darks). White gouache sparingly only.
**Wash order:**
1. Light pencil: door opening right third, board lines.
2. Wax-resist tally strokes in many 'hands' (varied angle/length) — BEFORE any wash.
3. Cedar wash Burnt Sienna + Quin Gold, dry-brush on cold-press tooth; tallies emerge pale.
4. Doorway: saturated blue snowfield, graded, paper white ridge.
5. Dry. Board seams + door frame darks.
6. Figures: two strokes, one hand reaching to add a mark.
7. Lift one fresh, brighter tally (today's).
**Keep white:** Snow ridge in doorway; today's mark.
**TJ north star:**
- PNW mountain huts: warm wood, cold blue beyond.
- BoS portraits: tiny figures still read as people — posture tells the story.
**Don't:** No numbers/dates · no legible names · no overworked grain. · no more than 2 pigments per wash.
**Done when:** The wall reads as many hands over years and the new mark joins them.
