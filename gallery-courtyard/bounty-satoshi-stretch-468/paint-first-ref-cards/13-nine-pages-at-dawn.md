# #13 Nine Pages at Dawn — Easel Card — PRIVATE
**Title / size / paper:** Nine Pages at Dawn · 15×22 in portrait (half sheet vertical) · Arches 300 lb cold press
**One-line beat:** Quiet completion and hope — one white paper, two empty chairs just pulled back.
**Thumbnail:**
- Lights: the paper rectangle (untouched), window behind-left
- Mid: dawn wall glow, table top, chairs
- Darks: chair shadows, cup shadows, window frame
**Palette:** New Gamboge + Quin Rose (wall glow) · Cobalt Blue (cup shadows) · Raw Sienna (chairs) · Phthalo Turquoise (window frame/sky) · Burnt Sienna (wood).
**Wash order:**
1. Light pencil: paper rectangle, two cups, two angled chair backs, window upper left.
2. Optional: nine faint clear-wax parallel lines on the paper.
3. Big wall wash Gamboge → Rose, saturated, around the paper and window.
4. Window frame/sky Phthalo Turquoise.
5. Dry. Table Burnt Sienna + Raw Sienna; chairs Raw Sienna, angled as just pulled out, controlled edges.
6. Cobalt shadows under cups; lift soft steam wisps.
**Keep white:** The paper rectangle — never touched, not one stroke.
**TJ north star:**
- Interior/room paintings: saturated room makes the white sing.
- BoS finale: emptiness as completion, not loss.
**Don't:** Title or symbol on the page · figures · greyed-down room.
**Done when:** The untouched white is the brightest, quietest thing on the wall.
