# #6 Halving Moon — Easel Card — PRIVATE
**Title / size / paper:** Halving Moon · 10×20 in vertical · 300 gsm cold press
**One-line beat:** Calm acceptance of a gentle, fixed schedule — four reflections, each half as bright.
**Thumbnail:**
- Lights: Top moon reflection (full white), then 3 descending, each a value darker
- Mid: Indigo tide-pool water, rock-edge rims
- Darks: Rocks framing pool, two seated silhouettes on rocks
**Palette:** Indanthrone/Ultramarine (indigo night) · Dioxazine Violet (depth) · Cadmium Yellow (moon cores) · tone Burnt Sienna (rocks w/ Ultramarine). White gouache sparingly only.
**Wash order:**
1. Light pencil: four stacked ellipses down the centre, rock outline.
2. Graded indigo wash top→bottom, wet-in-wet, around dry ellipses.
3. Dry. Second glaze darkening from lower third — rocks only.
4. Moons: #1 paper white + thin Cad Yellow rim; #2 pale yellow; #3 yellow+violet tint; #4 nearly the water value.
5. Couple: two one-stroke silhouettes on the rock, side by side.
6. Lift a soft ripple line under each moon.
7. Bone dry → optional single star spatter, sparse.
**Keep white:** Top moon core; thin glint rim on second moon.
**TJ north star:**
- Night blues (#26/#9): deep indigo, warm cores that glow.
- BoS portraits: tiny figures still read as people — posture tells the story.
**Don't:** No numbers, no coin/₿ shapes · no more than 2 pigments per wash · figures stay tiny. · no more than 2 pigments per wash.
**Done when:** The four moons read as a calm halving step — no explanation needed.
