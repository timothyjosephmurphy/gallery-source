# #1 Two Notebooks, One Kitchen Table — Easel Card — PRIVATE
**Title / size / paper:** Two Notebooks, One Kitchen Table · 11×15 in landscape (quarter sheet) · Arches 140 lb cold press
**One-line beat:** The first quiet "oh — you think this too?" — two coffee rings overlap across two pages.
**Thumbnail:**
- Lights: both open notebook pages (reserved paper), edge to edge center
- Mid: ochre table, hands, coffee cup
- Darks: cobalt cast shadows raking right from pen and cup; overlapping coffee-ring edges
**Palette:** Raw Sienna + Quin Gold (table) · Cobalt Blue (shadows) · Quin Rose (warm skin) · Burnt Sienna (coffee rings) · Ultramarine (ink lines).
**Wash order:**
1. Light pencil: two page rectangles touching, two ring circles overlapping across the gutter, hands at lower edge.
2. Optional: clear-wax tick in each notebook margin.
3. Table wet-in-wet Raw Sienna, drop Quin Gold for grain — paint around the pages.
4. Dry. Hands: Quin Rose + Raw Sienna, faces cropped at chin.
5. Coffee rings: damp circle, drop dilute Burnt Sienna, let edges bloom and dry hard.
6. Cobalt cast shadows raking right from pen and cup, one pass.
7. Rigger handwriting suggestion in Ultramarine; dry-brush knuckles and page edges.
**Keep white:** Both page rectangles (paint around, no masking) except rigger marks and rings.
**TJ north star:**
- Hands-and-tools intimacy: the story lives in fingers and paper.
- BoS portraits: warm ochre against cobalt, morning raking light.
**Don't:** Show faces · any screen · glowing ink · legible words.
**Done when:** The overlapping ring reads first, and the two pages read as a pair.
