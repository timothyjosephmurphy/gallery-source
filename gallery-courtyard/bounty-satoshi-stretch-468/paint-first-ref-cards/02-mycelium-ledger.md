# #2 Mycelium Ledger — Easel Card — PRIVATE
**Title / size / paper:** Mycelium Ledger · 11×15 in portrait · 300 gsm cold press
**One-line beat:** Wonder at a network nobody owns — roots trade trust beneath a sunlit trail.
**Thumbnail:**
- Lights: sunlit trail band (top third), glowing thread knots
- Mid: cedar trunks above, teal/gold thread web below
- Darks: loamy cutaway soil mass, root silhouettes
**Palette:** Quin Gold (sun + knots) · Phthalo Turquoise (threads) · Burnt Sienna (soil w/ Ultramarine) · Ultramarine · Sap-touch via Gold+Turquoise. White gouache = 3–4 knot sparks only.
**Wash order:**
1. Light pencil: horizon at 1/3, two trunks dropping into roots, knot spots.
2. Mask or reserve knot dots.
3. Top: Quin Gold wash, Turquoise+Gold greens for canopy; dry.
4. Bottom: wet Burnt Sienna+Ultramarine soil; drop Turquoise and Gold threads wet-in-wet.
5. Dry. Root shapes glazed darker, soft edges into soil.
6. Two tiny walkers on the trail, one stroke each.
7. Remove mask; tint knots Quin Gold, a gouache spark on 3–4.
**Keep white:** Knot dots; sunlit trail strip.
**TJ north star:**
- PNW forest light: gold through cedar.
- Nature metaphor reads before the idea does.
**Don't:** No literal nodes/diagrams · no crypto chrome · don't over-thread (mud).
**Done when:** The underground web glows warmer than the world above.
