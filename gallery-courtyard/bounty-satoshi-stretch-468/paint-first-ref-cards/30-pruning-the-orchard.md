# #30 Pruning the Orchard — Easel Card — PRIVATE
**Title / size / paper:** Pruning the Orchard · 12×12 in square · 300 gsm cold press
**One-line beat:** Order that is alive — small checks add up to one root.
**Thumbnail:**
- Lights: Bright spring sky + lifted blossoms
- Mid: Grass, ladders
- Darks: Central trunk, branch fan, figure silhouettes
**Palette:** Cerulean (sky) · Sap/Phthalo Green + Quin Gold (grass) · Burnt Sienna + Ultramarine (trunk) · Quin Rose (tag dots). White gouache sparingly only.
**Wash order:**
1. Light pencil: trunk, symmetrical branch fan, two ladders.
2. Sky Cerulean graded, light.
3. Grass band, one pass.
4. Dry. Trunk + branches dry-brush, converging to one trunk.
5. Blossoms: lift/reserve whites.
6. Tags: tiny warm Rose dots on branches.
7. Figures on ladders, 2 strokes each.
**Keep white:** Blossom whites.
**TJ north star:**
- Mycelium (#2) above ground: structure that breathes.
- BoS portraits: tiny figures still read as people — posture tells the story.
**Don't:** No text tags · no diagrams · no hard outlines. · no more than 2 pigments per wash.
**Done when:** Every branch reads as leading to one trunk.
