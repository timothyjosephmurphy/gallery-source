# #7 Twenty-One Million Seeds — Easel Card — PRIVATE
**Title / size / paper:** Twenty-One Million Seeds · 11×15 in portrait · 300 gsm cold press
**One-line beat:** Scarcity that feels like enough — an open palm of seeds before counted sunflower rows.
**Thumbnail:**
- Lights: low sun backlight rim on hand edge + sky behind hill
- Mid: sunflower hillside blooms (yellow/orange), sky gradient
- Darks: palm interior shadow, seeds, row furrows
**Palette:** New Gamboge (sunflowers) · Quin Gold (warm light) · Pyrrole Orange (bloom cores) · Burnt Sienna (seeds, skin shadow) · Ultramarine (furrow darks w/ Burnt Sienna).
**Wash order:**
1. Light pencil: large foreground hand bottom-centre, horizon high, row lines converging.
2. Sky→hill wet-in-wet Gamboge + Quin Gold; drop Orange for blooms; leave sun rim dry.
3. Dry fully. Row furrows: Ultramarine + Burnt Sienna, soft parallel strokes.
4. Hand: warm skin glaze (Quin Gold + touch Orange), crisp edges — the only sharp shape.
5. Palm shadow glaze, then seeds as small Burnt Sienna dots, hard edge.
**Keep white:** Rim light on hand edge; sun glow above hill.
**TJ north star:**
- El Salvador fields: saturated, hot, generous colour.
- BoS "Achievement": a small quiet gesture against abundance.
**Don't:** No face (hand only) · no coins/symbols · don't sharpen the hillside — blooms stay soft.
**Done when:** The palm reads sharp against a glowing soft hill and seeds feel countable.
