# #36 The Letter Returned, Rewritten — Easel Card — PRIVATE
**Title / size / paper:** The Letter Returned, Rewritten · 11×15 in portrait · 300 gsm cold press
**One-line beat:** Loss turned to renewal — a returned letter in shadow, the new page already in light.
**Thumbnail:**
- Lights: tall window shaft falling down the stair diagonal; Tanaka's fresh page
- Mid: stair treads, lit wall, Esperanza's figure
- Darks: stairwell shadow under the steps, the stamped envelope in her hands
**Palette:** Ultramarine (shadow) · Quin Rose (violet into shadow) · Lemon Yellow (light shaft) · tone Burnt Sienna (darks w/ Ultramarine). No gouache.
**Wash order:**
1. Light pencil: stair diagonal lower-left → upper-right, window top right, page placement on a lit step.
2. Mask nothing; paint around the shaft and the page shape.
3. Graded light shaft: Lemon at window fading to bare paper on the steps, wet-in-wet.
4. Dry. Cool shadow Ultramarine + Rose over the under-stair mass and left wall.
5. Second glaze on the deepest shadow only, Ultramarine + Burnt Sienna; envelope stays in this zone.
6. Figures loose: Esperanza seated in half-shadow, Tanaka bent over the page in light.
7. Lift a soft edge where the shaft meets the treads.
**Keep white:** The new page and the brightest stretch of the shaft.
**TJ north star:**
- Warsaw quiet: violet shadow, held breath, then light.
- BoS portraits: posture carries it — reading vs writing.
**Don't:** No hard architectural detail · no visible "returned" text or stamps rendered literally · no chain/crypto chrome.
**Done when:** The eye drops from the window to the white page, and the envelope sits calmly in shadow.
