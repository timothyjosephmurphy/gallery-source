# #11 The Pages Begin to Fuse — Easel Card — PRIVATE
**Title / size / paper:** The Pages Begin to Fuse · 9×12 in landscape · Arches 140 lb cold press (one sitting)
**One-line beat:** Bittersweet — ink bleeds across two notebooks into one sheet; names fade, the idea won't.
**Thumbnail:**
- Lights: two pages on the sill; gold edge light at right
- Mid: Seattle-grey window glass with rain streaks, sill
- Darks: window frame / wall left, ink bloom at the gutter
**Palette:** Ultramarine + Burnt Sienna (grey, mixed on paper) · Quin Gold (edge light) · Indigo-leaning Ultramarine (ink) · Quin Rose (one warm bleed).
**Wash order:**
1. Light pencil: window, sill line, two notebooks touching.
2. Window glass wet-in-wet Ultramarine + Burnt Sienna, warming to Quin Gold at right edge.
3. Dry. Lift or salt rain streaks on the glass only.
4. Notebook "text": damp Ultramarine ink lines on both pages.
5. Drag a wet brush across the gutter so both bloom together; drop one Quin Rose bleed. Accept the backrun.
6. Soft sill shadow. Stop early.
**Keep white:** Outer page margins and the right-edge gold highlight.
**TJ north star:**
- The watercolor process is the subject — water does the fusing.
- BoS book art: soft grey into gold.
**Don't:** Rescue blooms · legible words · hard outline around the sill.
**Done when:** You can't tell where one notebook's ink ends and the other's begins.
