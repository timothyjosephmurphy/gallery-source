# #31 The Family Album — Easel Card — PRIVATE
**Title / size / paper:** The Family Album · 11×15 in landscape · 300 gsm cold press
**One-line beat:** Belonging across time; nothing rewritten, everything remembered.
**Thumbnail:**
- Lights: Afternoon sun stripe across album
- Mid: Album pages, photo rectangles varied warm/cool
- Darks: Table edges, hand shadows
**Palette:** Quin Gold (sun stripe) · Burnt Sienna (hands, table) · Cobalt (cool photos) · Quin Rose (warm photos). White gouache sparingly only.
**Wash order:**
1. Light pencil: album spread, overlapping photo rectangles, three hands.
2. Table Burnt Sienna wash, around album.
3. Pages pale warm tint.
4. Photos: small varied rectangles, each tucked under the last's corner.
5. Hands: three generations — the subject; warm, soft.
6. Sun stripe: lift diagonal across spread.
7. Soft edges on any photo figures (no faces).
**Keep white:** Sun stripe.
**TJ north star:**
- Pages-into-lineage (#11/#14).
- BoS portraits: tiny figures still read as people — posture tells the story.
**Don't:** No legible faces in photos · no text · no hard edges. · no more than 2 pigments per wash.
**Done when:** The tucked-corner chain reads and the hands carry it.
