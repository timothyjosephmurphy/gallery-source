# #24 The Garden Adjusts — Easel Card — PRIVATE
**Title / size / paper:** The Garden Adjusts · 12×12 in square · 300 gsm cold press
**One-line beat:** Patient care in after-rain light — loosening, tightening; the pace holds however fast it grows.
**Thumbnail:**
- Lights: sky gaps, wet-leaf highlights, chart paper on shed
- Mid: back foliage mass, warm front leaves, hands
- Darks: shadowed vine interior behind the four hands (focal contrast)
**Palette:** Phthalo Blue GS (diluted — cool back greens w/ Lemon) · Hansa/Lemon Yellow (warm front greens) · Quin Rose (hands, sawtooth line) · tone Raw Sienna (skin w/ Rose, twine, shed).
**Wash order:**
1. Pencil trellis grid, four hands at the twine, shed chart.
2. Pale Lemon + Phthalo backdrop wet-in-wet; leave sky whites.
3. Dry. Leaves wet-on-dry: warm yellow-heavy front, cool back — masses, not counts.
4. Dark interior accents behind hands.
5. Hands/faces: Rose + Raw Sienna, two glazes.
6. Twine + tendrils, single rigger lines.
7. Chart: one Rose sawtooth line on pale paper.
8. Optional: sparse salt into a damp (not wet) passage.
**Keep white:** Sky gaps, rain sparkle on 5–7 leaves, chart paper.
**TJ north star:**
- PNW garden: bright Seattle-after-rain, saturated greens.
- BoS portraits: hands and faces lead; foliage serves them.
**Don't:** Phthalo straight from tube · leaf-by-leaf noodling · salt on soaking paper.
**Done when:** The four hands are the first thing you see and the greens still feel wet and lit.
