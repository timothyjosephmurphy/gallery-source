# #44 Nine Copies Drying in the Open Studio — Easel Card — PRIVATE
**Title / size / paper:** Nine Copies Drying in the Open Studio · 16×12 landscape in · 300 gsm cold press
**One-line beat:** Letting go: the work is out in the world and no longer theirs.
**Thumbnail:**
- Lights: brightest reserved note per watercolor notes (rain light clearing to gold)
- Mid: main atmospheric wash
- Darks: one pass for structure, framing the light
**Palette:** Cobalt Teal · Quin Gold · Burnt Sienna · Sap Green.
**Wash order:**
1. Light pencil: Eye-level across the room; pages a luminous horizontal band; open door at right with green garden beyond.
2. Reserve the key whites.
3. Overall atmospheric wash wet-in-wet, lighter toward the focal note.
4. Mid-value shapes while damp.
5. Dry fully. Darks one pass.
6. Accents: Reserve the nine pages white; window wash Cobalt Teal to Quin Gold; floor Burnt Sienna; door garden Sap Green.
7. Soften edges into the white.
**Keep white:** focal light until the final glaze.
