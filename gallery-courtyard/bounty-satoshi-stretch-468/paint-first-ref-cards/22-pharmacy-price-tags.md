# #22 The Pharmacy Price Tags (Remembered) — Easel Card — PRIVATE
**Title / size / paper:** The Pharmacy Price Tags (Remembered) · 11×15 in landscape · 300 gsm cold press
**One-line beat:** Tender grief held lightly — told with love, not doom.
**Thumbnail:**
- Lights: Window light, pale counter interior
- Mid: Jacaranda violet mass outside
- Darks: Counter edge, shelf shadows, small figure silhouettes
**Palette:** Dioxazine Violet + Cobalt (jacaranda) · Quin Gold (warm hand note) · Ultramarine + Burnt Sienna (soft darks). White gouache sparingly only.
**Wash order:**
1. Light pencil: window big right half, counter, two figure hands.
2. Pale interior wash, keep it light (not grim).
3. Jacaranda wet-in-wet Violet + Cobalt drops; let blooms form.
4. Soft faded memory edges — wet the border, drop colour.
5. Counter/shelf darks, one pass.
6. Grandmother's hand: warm Quin Gold note — focal warmth.
7. Sticker: tiny ordinary pale rectangle.
**Keep white:** Window light; tiny sticker.
**TJ north star:**
- Memory scenes: blooms, faded edges.
- BoS portraits: tiny figures still read as people — posture tells the story.
**Don't:** No legible prices · no faces · no doom palette. · no more than 2 pigments per wash.
**Done when:** Jacaranda huge and violet, the hand the warmest note.
