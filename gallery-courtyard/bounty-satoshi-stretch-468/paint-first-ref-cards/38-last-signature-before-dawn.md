# #38 The Last Signature Before Dawn — Easel Card — PRIVATE
**Title / size / paper:** The Last Signature Before Dawn · 12×16 in landscape · 300 gsm cold press
**One-line beat:** Quiet resolve before the Nine Pages — two hands on one pen, authorship dissolving into the shared name.
**Thumbnail:**
- Lights: fused-page center (paper white growing outward) + tiny candle flame
- Mid: pre-dawn room wash, lavender window band over Puget Sound, half-white notebooks
- Darks: table edge and figures, one pass, framing the page
**Palette:** Ultramarine (pre-dawn) · Quin Rose (lavender band, violet tint) · Quin Gold (candle, small and spent) · tone Burnt Sienna (darks w/ Ultramarine).
**Wash order:**
1. Light pencil: table, both hands on pen, notebooks, window behind.
2. Reserve fused-page center and candle flame.
3. Overall pre-dawn wash Ultramarine + Rose, wet-in-wet, lighter toward the page.
4. Window lavender band: Rose kissed into blue.
5. Dry fully. Table and figures one pass Ultramarine + Burnt Sienna.
6. Candle: Quin Gold, tiny and soft.
7. Soften notebook edges into the white.
**Keep white:** Page center throughout — it must be the brightest note; candle core until step 6.
**TJ north star:**
- Bookends #1: same kitchen table, now at dawn — hopeful, not night.
- Leads into #13 Nine Pages at Dawn: the white center is the handoff.
**Don't:** No more than 2 pigments per wash (mud) · don't lose the white center · no too-dark night · candle never competes with the page · no overworked faces.
**Done when:** The page center glows as the lightest thing in the room and the shared pen reads at a glance.
