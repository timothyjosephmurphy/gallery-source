# #3 The Generals at Low Tide — Easel Card — PRIVATE
**Title / size / paper:** The Generals at Low Tide · 10×30 in panoramic landscape · Arches 300 lb cold press
**One-line beat:** Tension resolving into shared certainty — lanterns agree across a bay despite one false light.
**Thumbnail:**
- Lights: horizon glow band + lantern dots (warm) and their lifted reflections
- Mid: dusk violet sky, tide pools, wet sand
- Darks: low rocks along the waterline, 3–4-stroke figures
**Palette:** Dioxazine Violet + Ultramarine (sky) · Phthalo Turquoise (tide pools; false lantern tint) · New Gamboge + Quin Rose (lanterns) · Burnt Sienna (rocks).
**Wash order:**
1. Light pencil: low horizon, rock line, 5–6 lantern positions.
2. Wax-resist dots for each lantern halo.
3. Graded wet-in-wet sky Violet → Ultramarine, warm Rose glow near horizon.
4. While damp, charge rocks Burnt Sienna + Ultramarine so they sit low and dark.
5. Dry. Tide pools Phthalo Turquoise; lift vertical reflection streaks with thirsty brush.
6. Drop Gamboge (+ touch Rose) into resisted dots; one lantern in turquoise-green, painted with care.
7. Figures 3–4 strokes each.
**Keep white:** Lantern halos (wax) and a thin horizon sliver.
**TJ north star:**
- El Zonte shoreline language at dusk; lanterns the only warm notes.
- Panoramic quiet — distance between rocks is the drama.
**Don't:** Lines linking lanterns (no network diagram) · symbols on lanterns · detailed figures.
**Done when:** The eye counts the lanterns, finds the odd one, and still feels agreement.
