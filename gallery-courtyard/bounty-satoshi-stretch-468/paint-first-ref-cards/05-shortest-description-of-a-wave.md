# #5 Shortest Description of a Wave — Easel Card — PRIVATE
**Title / size / paper:** Shortest Description of a Wave · 15×11 in landscape (diptych feel) · 300 gsm cold press
**One-line beat:** Joy of finding the essential — a busy wave on the left compresses to one clean stroke on the right.
**Thumbnail:**
- Lights: right half paper white; scattered page shapes on sand
- Mid: left wave layers, sand ground, soft sky
- Darks: deepest wave trough (left), the single ultramarine line (right)
**Palette:** Ultramarine (line + wave) · Phthalo Turquoise (wave body) · Raw Sienna/Quin Gold (sand) · Burnt Sienna (sand shadow w/ Ultramarine). No gouache.
**Wash order:**
1. Light pencil: centre split, page rectangles, one curve for the final line.
2. Left half only: wet-in-wet Turquoise + Ultramarine wave layers, let blooms happen.
3. Sand band: pale Quin Gold, paint around page shapes.
4. Dry fully. Second glaze on left trough + page cast shadows (Ultramarine + Burnt Sienna, light).
5. Right half: ONE loaded Ultramarine stroke, single pass, no touch-up.
**Keep white:** Entire right half except the line; page shapes on sand.
**TJ north star:**
- El Zonte surf: turquoise water energy on the left.
- Warsaw sketchbook: confident single-gesture line.
**Don't:** No second attempt at the final stroke (start a new sheet) · no detail on the right side · no text on pages.
**Done when:** Left feels full, right feels finished with one line, and the eye travels left→right.
