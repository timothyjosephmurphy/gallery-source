# #27 Her Mother's Lamp at the Shrine Gate — Easel Card — PRIVATE
**Title / size / paper:** Her Mother's Lamp at the Shrine Gate · 10×14 in portrait · 300 gsm cold press
**One-line beat:** Quiet inheritance — care that keeps going without being watched.
**Thumbnail:**
- Lights: Paper lamp (warmest, top-right eye point)
- Mid: Dusk sky, swept stone path
- Darks: Gate posts framing, long violet shadow, small sweeping figure
**Palette:** Ultramarine + Quin Rose (dusk violet) · Indian Yellow + Quin Gold (lamp) · Burnt Sienna + Ultramarine (gate posts). White gouache sparingly only.
**Wash order:**
1. Light pencil: two posts, lintel, lamp oval top-right, figure lower third.
2. Soft dusk glaze Ultramarine→Rose, around dry lamp.
3. Dry. Gate posts Burnt Sienna + Ultramarine, one pass.
4. Lamp: Indian Yellow core, drop Quin Gold — allow one warm bloom.
5. Long violet shadow across path from figure.
6. Figure: 3 strokes + broom line.
7. Lift a faint glow halo on adjacent post.
**Keep white:** Lamp paper until step 4.
**TJ north star:**
- Gentle memory — gratitude, not loss (vs #22).
- BoS portraits: tiny figures still read as people — posture tells the story.
**Don't:** No faces · no shadow-drama · no text on lamp/gate. · no more than 2 pigments per wash.
**Done when:** The lamp glows steady without anyone tending it.
