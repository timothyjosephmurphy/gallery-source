# #8 The Argument on the Ferry — Easel Card — PRIVATE
**Title / size / paper:** The Argument on the Ferry · 15×11 in landscape · 300 gsm cold press
**One-line beat:** Friction that sharpens — wind tears pages on a WA ferry deck; one page is caught by both hands.
**Thumbnail:**
- Lights: flying pages (negative space), the shared page at centre
- Mid: Puget Sound teal, Olympic peaks, sky
- Darks: diagonal deck rail/shadow, two figure silhouettes
**Palette:** Phthalo Turquoise (Sound) · Ultramarine (peaks, sky) · Neutral (Ultramarine + Burnt Sienna) for deck · Quin Rose (touch on peaks) · Burnt Sienna.
**Wash order:**
1. Light pencil: strong deck diagonal, two figures mid-frame, page shapes.
2. Sky: Ultramarine graded, dry-brush wind streaks.
3. Peaks: Ultramarine + touch Rose, soft lost edge.
4. Sound: Turquoise + Ultramarine around page whites.
5. Dry. Deck and rail neutral, one confident diagonal pass.
6. Figures: one stroke each, leaning into wind; hands meeting on the white page.
**Keep white:** All pages, especially the centre shared page.
**TJ north star:**
- PNW travel: teal water, cold bright light.
- BoS portraits: posture tells the argument, tiny figures.
**Don't:** No faces/detail · no text on pages · no more than 2 pigments per wash.
**Done when:** The diagonal feels windy and the shared page is the brightest thing.
