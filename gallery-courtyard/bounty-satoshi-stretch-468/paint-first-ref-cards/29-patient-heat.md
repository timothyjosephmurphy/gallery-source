# #29 Patient Heat — Easel Card — PRIVATE
**Title / size / paper:** Patient Heat (The Potter's Kiln) · 11×15 in landscape · 300 gsm cold press
**One-line beat:** Work you can't fake — an all-night kiln, two people waiting; proof-of-work as old craft.
**Thumbnail:**
- Lights: kiln core, rim-light edges on figures, bowl highlights
- Mid: kiln brick, moonlit bowl stack, ground
- Darks: night surround, figure silhouettes (value study — big dark, small hot light)
**Palette:** Ultramarine (night) · Quin Rose (violet tint; core edges) · Indian Yellow (core) · Quin Gold (core transition) · tone Burnt Sienna (brick; darks w/ Ultramarine). No black.
**Wash order:**
1. Pencil kiln mouth, potter + Tanaka (log in hand), bowl stack.
2. Night wash Ultramarine + touch Rose wet-in-wet, around dry core and rim edges.
3. Dry. Brick glaze Burnt Sienna.
4. Core: Indian Yellow, drop Quin Gold/Rose at rim — keep a dry ring.
5. Figures one-pass silhouettes, warm edge facing kiln.
6. Bowls: pale Ultramarine moonlight; lift highlights.
7. Deepen darks nearest the core only (one glaze).
**Keep white:** Kiln core centre, figure rim-light, 3–4 bowl lips.
**TJ north star:**
- Warsaw night: luminous night value structure.
- PNW garden: island, wood, time-as-pace kinship with #24.
**Don't:** Black or overmixed darks · machines/spark clichés · glow bleeding into night.
**Done when:** Squinting shows one hot light in a quiet dark, and both figures read as waiting together.
