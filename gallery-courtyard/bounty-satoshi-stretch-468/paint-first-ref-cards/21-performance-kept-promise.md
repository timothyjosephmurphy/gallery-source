# #21 The Performance That Kept Its Promise — Easel Card — PRIVATE
**Title / size / paper:** The Performance That Kept Its Promise · 10×14 in portrait · 300 gsm cold press
**One-line beat:** Awe turning into an idea — effort you can witness is effort you can trust.
**Thumbnail:**
- Lights: Indian Yellow light pool on performer + lifted chalk tallies
- Mid: Stage floor mid-warm grey, receding
- Darks: Black-box house, seats, tiny backlit Tanaka
**Palette:** Indian Yellow (light pool) · Ultramarine + Burnt Sienna (house darks, layered) · Quin Rose (touch in pool edge). White gouache sparingly only.
**Wash order:**
1. Light pencil: pool ellipse, floor perspective lines, performer gesture.
2. Reserve pool with soft (no masking) edge; Indian Yellow wet core, Rose at rim.
3. House darks: 2–3 glazes Ultramarine + Burnt Sienna, drying between.
4. Performer: one gesture, 2 strokes, mid-repetition.
5. Chalk tallies: lift rows with damp flat brush, receding.
6. Tanaka: one tiny backlit stroke in the seats.
7. Soften one edge where light meets dark.
**Keep white:** Pool core; chalk lifts.
**TJ north star:**
- BoS portraits: tiny figures read by posture.
- BoS portraits: tiny figures still read as people — posture tells the story.
**Don't:** No machinery · no faces · no numbers on floor. · no more than 2 pigments per wash.
**Done when:** The light pool feels like devotion and the tallies recede convincingly.
