# #14 Genesis Cedar — Easel Card — PRIVATE
**Title / size / paper:** Genesis Cedar · 15×11 in landscape · 300 gsm cold press
**One-line beat:** Long patience — one saturated green cedar sapling in a burned clearing; the couple tiny, walking away.
**Thumbnail:**
- Lights: light shafts through breaking clouds, highlight on sapling tip
- Mid: grey clearing ground, cloud masses
- Darks: charred stumps, distant treeline, couple silhouettes
**Palette:** Sap/Hooker's Green + Phthalo (sapling only) · Ultramarine + Burnt Sienna (charcoal greys) · Payne's-style neutral · Quin Gold (light shafts).
**Wash order:**
1. Light pencil: low horizon, sapling small right-of-centre, couple far left, shaft diagonals.
2. Sky wet-in-wet: neutral greys dropping Quin Gold where shafts fall; lift shafts while damp.
3. Ground: warm-to-cool grey, leave sapling shape white.
4. Dry. Charred stumps and treeline: dark neutral, dry-brush edges.
5. Couple: two tiny strokes, hands joined.
6. Last: sapling in pure saturated green — the only green on the sheet.
**Keep white:** Sapling shape until step 6; shaft paths.
**TJ north star:**
- PNW skies: dramatic cloud break.
- Restraint: tiny subject, huge space.
**Don't:** No other greens · no big figures/faces · don't overwork the sky.
**Done when:** The sapling is the first thing you see from across the room.
