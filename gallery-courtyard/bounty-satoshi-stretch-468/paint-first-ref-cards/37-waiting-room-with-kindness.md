# #37 The Waiting Room With Kindness — Easel Card — PRIVATE
**Title / size / paper:** The Waiting Room With Kindness · 11×15 in landscape · 300 gsm cold press
**One-line beat:** Patience made humane — everyone in the queue will be seen.
**Thumbnail:**
- Lights: open door to street light (right), the mango slice, folded notes
- Mid: turquoise walls, receding row of chairs
- Darks: seated figure masses, chair legs, door frame
**Palette:** Phthalo Turquoise + Lemon (walls, diluted) · Indian Yellow (mango, door light) · Quin Rose (clothing accents) · tone Burnt Sienna + Ultramarine (darks).
**Wash order:**
1. Light pencil: chair row receding left → door on right; child and elderly man at mid-left.
2. Door light: Indian Yellow wet, fading out to paper at its edge.
3. Walls: dilute Phthalo + Lemon, around the door and figure shapes.
4. Dry. Floor: thin Burnt Sienna glaze, warmer near the door.
5. Figures: varied gestures, one or two strokes each; Rose accents on 3 garments max.
6. Mango slice: pure Indian Yellow, crisp edge, in the child's outstretched hand.
7. Darks last: chair legs and door frame, Ultramarine + Burnt Sienna.
**Keep white:** Folded notes, sparkle in the doorway.
**TJ north star:**
- El Salvador brightness: saturated street, cool calm interior.
- BoS portraits: people first — gesture over faces.
**Don't:** No clinical green-grey · no numbers/screens on walls · no tech or crypto chrome.
**Done when:** The mango-slice gesture reads first, and the room feels unhurried.
