# Wave6 Paint-First Watercolor Notes #39–#44 — PRIVATE
All: 300 gsm cold press, bright saturated colour for gray Seattle light, max 2 pigments per wash, painterly not cartoon, no logos.

## #40 The Oyster Bed Waits
- **Paper/size:** 12×16 landscape in, 300 gsm cold press
- **Palette:** Cobalt Teal · Burnt Sienna · Quin Rose · Ultramarine
- **Light:** flat silver low-tide light
- **Technique:** Wet-in-wet sky mirrored in flats; Cobalt Teal channels; racks dry-brush Burnt Sienna; reserve reflections.
- **Angle/emotion:** low horizon — stillness
- **Avoid:** mud, cartoon figures, any visible logo or screen, hero lighting on a single person.
