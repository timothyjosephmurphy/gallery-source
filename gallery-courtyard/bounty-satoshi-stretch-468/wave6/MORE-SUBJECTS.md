# Bounty of Satoshi — Act II Wave6 Subjects #39–#44 — PRIVATE

## #39 Cairns on the Ridge
- **Motif:** Hurricane Ridge trail in late light: a line of small stone cairns, each added by a different hiker; Tanaka sets one stone and walks on without looking back.
- **Emotional beat:** Belonging without credit: the path is marked by everyone, owned by no one.
- **Composition:** Wide landscape, cairns receding up a diagonal ridge to bright sky; figures tiny at lower right, already turned away.
- **Extends:** #17 Snowpack Ledger, #23 Messengers Over the Pass
- **Act II beat:** Post-release ripple: each confirmation stacks on the last; no single builder.
- **Watercolor notes:** Granulating Ultramarine + Burnt Sienna stones; Quin Gold sky band; reserve the top cairn's rim light.

## #40 The Oyster Bed Waits
- **Motif:** Willapa Bay at low tide: Esperanza crouched beside oyster racks, a slow upper layer growing on the old shell bed; Tanaka's boots in the mud beside a tide stake.
- **Emotional beat:** Second-layer patience: the fast thing rests on the slow thing.
- **Composition:** Low horizon, racks in parallel bands over a mirror-wet mudflat; one pale sky reflected in tide channels.
- **Extends:** #18 Tide Table on the Fridge, #29 Patient Heat
- **Act II beat:** Building above the base layer without changing it.
- **Watercolor notes:** Wet-in-wet sky mirrored in flats; Cobalt Teal channels; racks dry-brush Burnt Sienna; reserve reflections.

## #41 Two Keys on One Hook
- **Motif:** A Warsaw apartment entry in morning sun: one old brass hook by the door holding two keys on a single red thread; coats, a window of courtyard chestnut.
- **Emotional beat:** Keys kept: trust held quietly at home, not in a vault of fear.
- **Composition:** Close vertical still life, hook at golden-section; sunlight raking across plaster wall, chestnut leaves outside.
- **Extends:** #25 Gift of a Key, #35 Seed Packet in the Garden
- **Act II beat:** Self-custody shared by two, quietly ordinary.
- **Watercolor notes:** Warm plaster Quin Gold + Rose glaze; keys Burnt Sienna with lifted highlights; red thread Pyrrol-type Quin Rose saturated.

## #42 The Bell Rope in the Plaza
- **Motif:** San Salvador plaza at noon: a church bell rings and the whole crowd looks up toward the sound; the ringer's hands on the rope are only a shadow in the doorway.
- **Emotional beat:** Public broadcast without hero worship: the message matters, not the messenger.
- **Composition:** Wide plaza, faces tilted up, bright saturated stalls; doorway a dark vertical at left edge, rope a thin line.
- **Extends:** #13 Nine Pages at Dawn, #12 A Name That Belongs to No One
- **Act II beat:** Release heard by everyone; author unseen.
- **Watercolor notes:** Strong noon sun: reserve white sky and plaza paving; saturated awnings Quin Rose / Cobalt Teal; doorway one dark pass.

## #43 The Kelp Forest Rejoins
- **Motif:** Underwater off Cape Town's Atlantic coast: a torn kelp strand drifts back and tangles into the standing forest; light shafts fall through green-gold water.
- **Emotional beat:** Orphan recovery: what broke away is gathered back into the larger whole.
- **Composition:** Vertical underwater view, shafts from top-right, drifting strand crossing to the dense left stand.
- **Extends:** #2 Mycelium Ledger, #36 Letter Returned, Rewritten
- **Act II beat:** The network heals; stray work rejoins the longest chain.
- **Watercolor notes:** Wet-in-wet Sap Green + Quin Gold water; lift light shafts with damp flat; kelp glazes Burnt Sienna over gold.

## #44 Nine Copies Drying in the Open Studio
- **Motif:** Esperanza's Seattle studio after release: nine printed pages pinned to a line in a bright window, door ajar, two coats gone from the hook; rain light turning gold.
- **Emotional beat:** Letting go: the work is out in the world and no longer theirs.
- **Composition:** Eye-level across the room; pages a luminous horizontal band; open door at right with green garden beyond.
- **Extends:** #13 Nine Pages at Dawn, #38 Last Signature Before Dawn
- **Act II beat:** Post-release coda: the authors step out of frame.
- **Watercolor notes:** Reserve the nine pages white; window wash Cobalt Teal to Quin Gold; floor Burnt Sienna; door garden Sap Green.
