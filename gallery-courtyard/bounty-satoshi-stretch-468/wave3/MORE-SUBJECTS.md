# Bounty of Satoshi — Act II Wave3 Subjects #21–#26 — PRIVATE
Same shape as wave2 (Motif / Emotional beat / Composition / Extends / Act II beat / Watercolor notes). No duplication of #1–#20.

## 21. The Performance That Kept Its Promise
- **Motif:** Esperanza's MFA thesis performance in a small black-box studio: she repeats one gesture for an hour, each repetition chalk-marked on the floor; Tanaka in the back row, notebook open, stops writing.
- **Emotional beat:** Awe turning into an idea — effort you can witness is effort you can trust.
- **Composition:** Vertical, low stage-level view; warm pool of Indian Yellow light on her, rows of chalk tallies receding in perspective; Tanaka tiny and backlit in the dark seats.
- **Extends:** Esperanza as artist; seeds proof-of-work as visible devotion.
- **Act II beat:** Origin of proof-of-work as human performance, not machinery.
- **Watercolor notes:** Reserve the light pool with masking-free soft edge; dark house in layered Ultramarine + Burnt Sienna glazes; chalk marks lifted with damp flat brush.

## 22. The Pharmacy Price Tags (Remembered)
- **Motif:** A memory scene in San Salvador: young Esperanza holding her grandmother's hand at a pharmacy counter while a clerk re-stickers a medicine box; outside the window, a jacaranda in full bloom.
- **Emotional beat:** Tender grief held lightly — why the supply cap matters to her, told with love not doom.
- **Composition:** Horizontal, window light dominant; the new price sticker small and ordinary, the jacaranda huge and violet. Grandmother's hand the focal warmth.
- **Extends:** El Salvador; inflation/medicine backstory.
- **Act II beat:** Motive for the 21M equation — money that doesn't quietly shrink a grandmother's care.
- **Watercolor notes:** Soft, slightly faded memory edges (bloom-friendly wet edges); jacaranda in Dioxazine Violet + Cobalt blossoms dropped wet; keep the counter interior pale, not grim.

## 23. Messengers Over the Pass
- **Motif:** Inland, not sea: three riders on different ridges of the Cascades at dusk, each carrying the same folded note, lit by signal fires that agree; one fire on a far ridge burns the wrong color and the riders keep their course.
- **Emotional beat:** Steadiness — trust built from agreement across distance.
- **Composition:** Wide panoramic, layered ridges cool-to-warm; three small riders, four fires, the odd fire in Quinacridone Magenta vs Cadmium Orange majority.
- **Extends:** Byzantine generals moved inland; companion to #3 and #15 without coast/river.
- **Act II beat:** BFT — two-thirds honest is enough to carry the message.
- **Watercolor notes:** Ridges as successive flat glazes, lightest first; fires as tiny reserved whites then dropped color; avoid overworking riders (three strokes each).

## 24. The Garden Adjusts
- **Motif:** Tanaka and Esperanza re-spacing a community pea-patch trellis in Seattle after two weeks of fast growth: some ties loosened, some tightened, a hand-drawn chart of "every two weeks" pinned to the shed.
- **Emotional beat:** Patient care — rhythm over control.
- **Composition:** Square, mid-close; vines in sap/phthalo greens, bright Seattle-after-rain light; their four hands at the twine; chart with a gentle sawtooth line.
- **Extends:** Seattle neighborhood; supply/time themes.
- **Act II beat:** Difficulty adjustment — the system keeps its pace no matter how fast the growth.
- **Watercolor notes:** Wet-on-dry leaves with hard sunny edges; rain sparkle via salt sparingly; twine in single fine rigger lines.

## 25. Gift of a Key, Cape Town
- **Motif:** On a Bo-Kaap stoop, Esperanza hands a stranger's child a small brass key on a string, and the child copies a word from her notebook onto their own palm.
- **Emotional beat:** Generosity — something given directly, person to person, no one in between.
- **Composition:** Vertical, saturated Bo-Kaap house walls (turquoise, pink, yellow) as color blocks; two hands meeting at center; Table Mountain soft in the gap.
- **Extends:** Cape Town travel; peer-to-peer.
- **Act II beat:** Peer-to-peer and self-custody — your key, your word.
- **Watercolor notes:** Flat bold wall washes with clean dry edges; skin tones glazed warm; brass key with a white reserve highlight.

## 26. A Stranger Nods Back
- **Motif:** Late night in Warsaw, a café window: Tanaka sees across the square a stranger at another lit window checking the same printed page, who looks up and nods once.
- **Emotional beat:** Quiet joy — the first time someone you've never met confirms your work.
- **Composition:** Horizontal, two warm windows across a blue-violet snowy square; tram lines leading eye between them; small figures, big space.
- **Extends:** Warsaw; companion to #9 lanterns without the chain.
- **Act II beat:** First confirmation from a stranger — the network begins.
- **Watercolor notes:** Night as graded Indanthrone/Ultramarine wash; windows reserved then Indian Yellow + Quin Gold; snow dots by spatter of white gouache only at the end.
