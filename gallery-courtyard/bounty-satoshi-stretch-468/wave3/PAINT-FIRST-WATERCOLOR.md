## #26 A Stranger Nods Back
- **Palette:** Ultramarine Blue, Quinacridone Rose, Indian Yellow, Quinacridone Gold; tone: Burnt Sienna (mix neutrals/darks with Ultramarine). White gouache only for snow.
- **Paper:** 300 gsm cold press, quarter sheet 11×15 in (landscape).
- **Steps:** 1) Light pencil: two windows, tram lines. 2) Reserve windows (dry edges). 3) Graded sky→square wash Ultramarine + touch Rose, wet-in-wet. 4) Dry; second glaze for building masses. 5) Windows: Indian Yellow, drop Quin Gold at bottom. 6) Figures in windows: one-stroke Burnt Sienna/Ultramarine silhouettes. 7) Tram lines with rigger, lifted glints. 8) Gouache snow spatter last.
- **Pitfalls:** Muddy night from overmixing (keep 2 pigments max per wash); losing window whites; figures too big — space is the story; don't spatter before fully dry.

## #24 The Garden Adjusts
- **Palette:** Phthalo Blue (GS), Hansa/Lemon Yellow, Quinacridone Rose; tone: Raw Sienna. Sap green optional.
- **Paper:** 300 gsm cold press, 12×12 in square (cut from half sheet).
- **Steps:** 1) Pencil trellis grid + hands. 2) Pale Lemon + Phthalo backdrop wash, wet-in-wet, leave sky whites. 3) Dry; leaves wet-on-dry, warm (yellow-heavy) front, cool back. 4) Hands: Rose + Raw Sienna glazes, two layers. 5) Twine and tendrils with rigger. 6) Chart on shed: pale paper, one Rose sawtooth line. 7) Optional sparse salt for after-rain sparkle.
- **Pitfalls:** Phthalo overpowering (dilute heavily); too many leaves — suggest masses; salt on too-wet paper blows out; keep faces/hands people-first, not crowded by foliage.
