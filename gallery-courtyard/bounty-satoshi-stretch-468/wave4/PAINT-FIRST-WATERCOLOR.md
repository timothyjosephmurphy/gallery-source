## #29 Patient Heat
- **Palette:** Ultramarine Blue, Quinacridone Rose, Indian Yellow, Quinacridone Gold; tone: Burnt Sienna (darks = Ultramarine + Burnt Sienna).
- **Paper:** 300 gsm cold press, quarter sheet 11×15 in (landscape).
- **Steps:** 1) Pencil kiln mouth, two figures, bowl stack. 2) Reserve kiln core and rim-light edges. 3) Night wash Ultramarine + touch Rose, wet-in-wet. 4) Dry; glaze kiln brick Burnt Sienna. 5) Core: Indian Yellow, drop Quin Gold/Rose at edges. 6) Figures one-pass silhouettes, warm edge left. 7) Bowls: pale Ultramarine moonlight, lift highlights.
- **Pitfalls:** Mud from overmixing (2 pigments/wash); losing the core white; heat glow bleeding into night — keep a dry ring; no machines/sparks clichés.

## #32 Wifi Hut at El Zonte
- **Palette:** Phthalo Blue (GS), Hansa/Lemon Yellow, Quinacridone Rose; tone: Raw Sienna. Ultramarine for dusk shadow.
- **Paper:** 300 gsm cold press, 12×16 in (landscape).
- **Steps:** 1) Pencil horizon low, hut, figure cluster. 2) Mask/reserve router dot + surf foam. 3) Graded sky Lemon → Rose → Phthalo, wet-in-wet. 4) Sea: Phthalo + Rose, dry-brush sparkle. 5) Hut thatch Raw Sienna strokes. 6) Figures warm Rose/Raw Sienna, people-first. 7) Router dot: tiny Quin Gold edge; first star lifted.
- **Pitfalls:** Phthalo overpowering; overworked sky; screens/logos detail (keep abstract); figures stiff — gesture strokes.
