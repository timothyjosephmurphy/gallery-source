# Bounty of Satoshi — Act II Wave4 Subjects #27–#32 — PRIVATE

## #27 Her Mother's Lamp at the Shrine Gate
- **Motif:** Tanaka sweeps a small shrine gate at dusk; a paper lamp his mother once tended glows on its own, steady.
- **Emotional beat:** Quiet inheritance — care that keeps going without needing to be watched.
- **Composition:** Vertical; gate posts frame Tanaka small at lower third; lamp the warmest value near the top-right eye point; long violet shadow.
- **Extends:** #1 Two Notebooks (where his patience comes from); new angle on memory (vs #22 grief) — gratitude, not loss.
- **Act II beat:** Origin of the "keep it running honestly" instinct.
- **Watercolor notes:** Soft dusk glaze, reserved lamp paper, one warm bloom; no faces in shadow-drama — gentle.

## #28 The Friend Who Gets It (Café in San Salvador)
- **Motif:** Esperanza sketches the idea on a napkin; her old friend Marisol laughs, grabs the pen, and draws the next box.
- **Emotional beat:** Relief of being understood for the first time.
- **Composition:** Three-quarter table view; two women leaning in, napkin center; bright window light, papel picado colour above.
- **Extends:** #8 Argument on the Ferry (from argument to recognition); peer verification as friendship.
- **Act II beat:** The idea survives contact with another mind.
- **Watercolor notes:** Saturated warm daylight; hands and smiles first; napkin lines kept as simple pencil—no diagrams legible.

## #29 Patient Heat (The Potter's Kiln)
- **Motif:** A potter on a Washington island tends a wood kiln all night; Tanaka feeds it a log and waits with her.
- **Emotional beat:** Work you can't fake — time and heat made visible.
- **Composition:** Night horizontal; kiln mouth orange core, two figures rim-lit; stack of finished bowls cooling in moonlight.
- **Extends:** #21 Performance (visible effort) and #24 Garden (pace); mining as craft, no machines.
- **Act II beat:** Proof-of-work found in an older human practice.
- **Watercolor notes:** Reserve the core then drop Indian Yellow/Rose; cool surround; avoid black.

## #30 Pruning the Orchard (Root of Many Branches)
- **Motif:** Esperanza and Tanaka prune an apple orchard in the Skagit; each branch tagged, all leading to one trunk.
- **Emotional beat:** Order that is alive — small checks add up to one sure thing.
- **Composition:** Square; one central tree, branches fanning symmetrically; two figures on ladders; bright spring sky.
- **Extends:** #2 Mycelium Ledger (below ground → above); Merkle tree as orchard.
- **Act II beat:** Summarize many facts into one root you can trust.
- **Watercolor notes:** Branch structure dry-brush; blossoms lifted/whites; keep tags as tiny warm dots, not text.

## #31 The Family Album (Timechain)
- **Motif:** Esperanza's grandmother turns album pages in Santa Ana; each photo tucked under the corner of the last.
- **Emotional beat:** Belonging across time; nothing rewritten, everything remembered.
- **Composition:** Overhead-angled table; album spread, three generations of hands; afternoon stripe of sun.
- **Extends:** #11 Pages Begin to Fuse (pages → lineage); #14 Genesis Cedar (rings → photos).
- **Act II beat:** The ledger as a shared family history.
- **Watercolor notes:** Photos as small rectangles of varied cool/warm; hands are the subject; soft edges on faces in photos.

## #32 The Wifi Hut at El Zonte
- **Motif:** A palm-thatched surf hut, one old router blinking; kids and surfers check scores on a single shared laptop at sunset.
- **Emotional beat:** Hope that starts tiny and local.
- **Composition:** Horizontal beach; hut left third, surf breaking right; warm figures clustered; first star overhead.
- **Extends:** #9 Lanterns / #26 Stranger (first lights → a first gathering place); El Salvador travel.
- **Act II beat:** The earliest node as a community porch.
- **Watercolor notes:** Saturated sunset graded wash; router light a single dot of reserved white + gold; no logos/screens detail.
