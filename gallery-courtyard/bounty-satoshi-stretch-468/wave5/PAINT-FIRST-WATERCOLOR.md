## #34 Fingerprint of a Leaf
- **Palette:** Sap Green, Indian Yellow, Quinacridone Rose, Phthalo Blue (GS); tone: Burnt Sienna (print, knuckle shadows).
- **Paper:** 300 gsm cold press, quarter sheet 11×15 in (portrait).
- **Steps:** 1) Pencil hand, leaf, print outline. 2) Reserve vein network and dapple spots. 3) Leaf wet-in-wet Indian Yellow → Sap Green, Rose at stem. 4) Dry; lift veins with damp flat. 5) Skin: Rose + Indian Yellow, cool Phthalo shadow under fingers. 6) Print: dry-brush Burnt Sienna on rough tooth. 7) Dapple: pale Phthalo glaze around reserved light.
- **Pitfalls:** Green turning olive/mud (keep 2 pigments); over-literal veins; hand stiff; no hex/code overlays.

## #38 Last Signature Before Dawn
- **Palette:** Ultramarine Blue, Quinacridone Rose, Quinacridone Gold; tone: Burnt Sienna (darks = Ultramarine + Burnt Sienna).
- **Paper:** 300 gsm cold press, 12×16 in (landscape).
- **Steps:** 1) Pencil table, both hands on pen, notebooks, window. 2) Reserve fused-page center and candle flame. 3) Overall pre-dawn wash Ultramarine + Rose, wet-in-wet, lighter toward page. 4) Window lavender band, Rose kissed into blue. 5) Dry; table and figures one-pass Ultramarine + Burnt Sienna. 6) Candle Quin Gold, tiny and soft. 7) Final: soften notebook edges into white.
- **Pitfalls:** Losing the white center; too-dark night (it's dawn, keep hopeful); candle too bright competing with page; faces overworked.
