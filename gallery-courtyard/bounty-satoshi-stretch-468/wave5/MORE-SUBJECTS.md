# Bounty of Satoshi — Act II Wave5 Subjects #33–#38 — PRIVATE

## #33 Disagreement Over Tea (The Softfork Kettle)
- **Motif:** Tanaka and Esperanza at a rain-streaked Seattle window, one kettle, two cups; both notebooks open to opposite pages, a single shared margin note bridging them.
- **Emotional beat:** First real disagreement about a rule change, resolved without anyone leaving: the old rule still holds, the new one only narrows it.
- **Composition:** Low eye level across the table; kettle steam rises as a vertical seam dividing then joining the two halves; window light from left.
- **Extends:** #8 Argument on the Ferry (now indoors, softer), #28 Friend Who Gets It.
- **Act II beat:** Consensus is chosen, not imposed: backward-compatible agreement.
- **Watercolor notes:** Cool grey-blue window wash, warm steam reserved white; cups Quinacridone Rose glaze; let both halves share one wet edge.

## #34 Fingerprint of a Leaf
- **Motif:** Esperanza's palm pressing a bigleaf maple leaf to paper in a Cape Town botanical garden; the vein print is unique, small, and unforgeable.
- **Emotional beat:** Wonder: something huge reduced to one honest mark.
- **Composition:** Tight overhead close-up; hand and leaf fill frame, print emerging below; dappled midday light.
- **Extends:** #5 Shortest Description of a Wave (Kolmogorov), #10 Hands That Verify.
- **Act II beat:** Hashing as identity: any change to the leaf, a new print.
- **Watercolor notes:** Sap Green + Indian Yellow leaf, veins lifted with damp brush; print in dry-brush Burnt Sienna; skin tones warm and glowing.

## #35 Seed Packet in the Garden (Cold Storage)
- **Motif:** Tanaka kneeling in a PNW backyard bed at golden hour, burying a hand-folded paper seed packet beside a marked stone; Esperanza holds the trowel and a hand-drawn map.
- **Emotional beat:** Trust in the future: something kept safely, out of reach, waiting for spring.
- **Composition:** Three-quarter view, low sun behind figures, long shadows pointing to the stone; packet the brightest note.
- **Extends:** #7 Twenty-One Million Seeds, #24 Garden Adjusts.
- **Act II beat:** Self-custody as patience and care, not fear.
- **Watercolor notes:** Rich soil Burnt Sienna + Ultramarine granulating; reserve packet white; backlit hair Quin Gold rim.

## #36 The Letter Returned, Rewritten (Orphan Block)
- **Motif:** In a Warsaw post-office stairwell, Esperanza reads an envelope stamped "returned"; on the step beside her, Tanaka is already writing the next version on fresh paper.
- **Emotional beat:** Loss turned to renewal, nothing wasted, the work rejoins the longer chain.
- **Composition:** Diagonal staircase, tall window light pouring down; unopened letter in shadow, new page in light.
- **Extends:** #9 Chain of Lanterns, Warsaw; #31 Family Album.
- **Act II beat:** Orphaned work and reorg: the longest honest chain wins, gracefully.
- **Watercolor notes:** Cool shadow Ultramarine + Rose; light shaft graded Lemon to white paper; avoid hard architectural detail.

## #37 The Waiting Room With Kindness (Mempool)
- **Motif:** A bright clinic-style waiting room in San Salvador: villagers holding small folded notes; a child offers a mango slice to an elderly man; Esperanza sorts notes by who has waited, gently.
- **Emotional beat:** Patience made humane: everyone will be seen.
- **Composition:** Wide interior, row of chairs receding; open door to saturated street light; figures varied in gesture.
- **Extends:** #22 Pharmacy Price Tags, #32 Wifi Hut.
- **Act II beat:** Mempool as a fair queue: priority and grace together.
- **Watercolor notes:** Turquoise walls (Phthalo + Lemon, diluted), mango Indian Yellow; loose figure gestures, people-first.

## #38 The Last Signature Before Dawn
- **Motif:** Kitchen table again (bookends #1), pre-dawn blue; both hands on one pen above the final page, the two notebooks already half-white, pages fusing at the seam.
- **Emotional beat:** Quiet resolve and hope just before the Nine Pages.
- **Composition:** Overhead three-quarter; window behind shows first lavender band over Puget Sound; single candle stub nearly out.
- **Extends:** #11 Pages Begin to Fuse; leads into #13 Nine Pages at Dawn.
- **Act II beat:** The decision to release: authorship dissolving into the shared name.
- **Watercolor notes:** Ultramarine + Rose pre-dawn, candle Quin Gold small and spent; let paper white grow toward the page center.
