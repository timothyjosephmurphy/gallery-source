# Bounty of Satoshi — Act II Painting Stretch (#468)
Private invent pack. Watercolor / watercolor pastel on Arches cold press. Bright, saturated, Seattle-light palette. People first; Bitcoin as patient, verified effort.

---

## 1. Two Notebooks, One Kitchen Table
- **Motif:** Tanaka and Esperanza at a worn table, two open notebooks edge to edge, coffee rings overlapping across both pages.
- **Emotional beat:** The first quiet "oh — you think this too?"
- **Composition:** 3/4 overhead, morning window light raking from the left; hands and pages fill 2/3 of frame, faces cropped at the chin. Warm ochre table, cobalt shadows.
- **Extends:** BoS portraits; TJ's hands-and-tools intimacy.
- **Act II beat:** Their separate ideas first touch on paper.

## 2. Mycelium Ledger
- **Motif:** Forest floor cross-section: fungal threads linking cedar roots, each node a tiny glowing knot; two figures walk the trail above.
- **Emotional beat:** Wonder at a network nobody owns.
- **Composition:** Split horizon — top 1/3 sunlit PNW trail, bottom 2/3 dark loamy cutaway with wet-in-wet threads in gold and teal.
- **Extends:** PNW forests; nature metaphors.
- **Act II beat:** They realize trust can be distributed like roots.

## 3. The Generals at Low Tide
- **Motif:** Several fishermen on separate rocks signaling by lantern across a bay, agreeing when to cast despite one false light.
- **Emotional beat:** Tension resolving into shared certainty.
- **Composition:** Wide panoramic, dusk violet sky, lanterns as warm dots; one lantern deliberately off-color. Low angle from waterline.
- **Extends:** El Zonte at Dawn/Sunrise shoreline language.
- **Act II beat:** Byzantine fault tolerance clicks through a human scene.

## 4. Esperanza Counting Rain
- **Motif:** Esperanza under an awning tallying raindrops into a puddle, notebook dotted with marks.
- **Emotional beat:** Patience as a form of love.
- **Composition:** Eye level, Seattle overcast turned luminous; puddle reflects saturated neon and sky. Figure small at right third, rain as lifted-out lines.
- **Extends:** PNW light; portrait series.
- **Act II beat:** She frames proof-of-work as time no one can fake.

## 5. Shortest Description of a Wave
- **Motif:** Tanaka sketching one wave over and over, each drawing simpler, until a single line remains.
- **Emotional beat:** Joy of finding the essential.
- **Composition:** Diptych feel: left a busy, layered wave; right one clean ultramarine stroke on white. Pages scattered on sand.
- **Extends:** El Zonte surf; Warsaw sketchbook.
- **Act II beat:** Kolmogorov complexity — truth is what can't be compressed further.

## 6. Halving Moon
- **Motif:** A tide pool reflecting a moon through four nights, each reflection half the previous one's brightness; the couple sit on the rocks.
- **Emotional beat:** Calm acceptance of a gentle, fixed schedule.
- **Composition:** Vertical 24×48, stacked reflections descending; deep indigo with glowing cadmium highlights.
- **Extends:** Nature metaphors; Cape Town coastline.
- **Act II beat:** The supply equation as something like a tide chart.

## 7. Twenty-One Million Seeds
- **Motif:** Esperanza's palm open, a few seeds; behind her a hillside of finite sunflowers, counted rows.
- **Emotional beat:** Scarcity that feels like enough.
- **Composition:** Close foreground hand (sharp), mid/back hillside wet-in-wet blooms in saturated yellow/orange. Low sun backlight.
- **Extends:** El Salvador fields; BoS "Achievement".
- **Act II beat:** A fixed cap becomes a promise, not a limit.

## 8. The Argument on the Ferry
- **Motif:** The two disagreeing on a Washington State ferry deck, wind tearing notebook pages; one page caught by both hands.
- **Emotional beat:** Friction that sharpens rather than breaks. (The edgy one.)
- **Composition:** Diagonal deck, strong wind lines, Puget Sound teal and Olympic peaks; figures mid-frame, pages as white negative space.
- **Extends:** PNW; travel scenes.
- **Act II beat:** They stress-test the design against each other.

## 9. Chain of Lanterns, Warsaw
- **Motif:** Night street, a line of paper lanterns each tied to the last, the couple hanging a new one.
- **Emotional beat:** Adding your small part to something that lasts.
- **Composition:** Receding perspective, warm lanterns against cool blue-violet; figures silhouetted near the foreground.
- **Extends:** Warsaw Bitcoin Film Festival sketches (Syrenka, old town).
- **Act II beat:** Blocks as linked, verified, append-only light.

## 10. Hands That Verify
- **Motif:** Grandmother in a Cape Town market weighing fruit on a brass scale while Tanaka watches and takes notes.
- **Emotional beat:** Respect for ordinary honesty.
- **Composition:** Close mid shot, saturated market colors, scale centered as the visual anchor; dappled sunlight.
- **Extends:** Cape Town / @AdoptingBTC travel; portraits.
- **Act II beat:** Verification belongs to everyone, not authorities.

## 11. The Pages Begin to Fuse
- **Motif:** Two notebooks laid on a windowsill in rain; ink bleeding across both into one sheet.
- **Emotional beat:** Bittersweet — their names will fade, the idea won't.
- **Composition:** Tight still life, wet-into-wet blooms used literally; soft Seattle grey turning gold at the edge.
- **Extends:** Watercolor process itself; BoS book art.
- **Act II beat:** The notebooks start becoming one document.

## 12. A Name That Belongs to No One
- **Motif:** The couple writing a pen name in wet sand as the tide comes in.
- **Emotional beat:** Selfless, tender humility.
- **Composition:** High angle looking down, letters half-erased by foam; their footprints leading off frame. Pastel sunrise.
- **Extends:** El Zonte shoreline; Berlín mural sensibility.
- **Act II beat:** They choose to sign as Satoshi Nakamoto.

## 13. Nine Pages at Dawn
- **Motif:** A single white paper on a table, morning light; two cups, two chairs, both empty — just pulled back.
- **Emotional beat:** Quiet completion; hope.
- **Composition:** Eye level, strong window light, the white of the paper as untouched white of Arches; everything around saturated.
- **Extends:** Interior/room paintings; BoS finale imagery.
- **Act II beat:** The white paper exists; Act II closes.

## 14. Genesis Cedar
- **Motif:** One new cedar sapling in a burned clearing, the couple far off, walking away hand in hand.
- **Emotional beat:** Long patience; a future they won't see in full.
- **Composition:** Wide, sapling small but most saturated green; dramatic PNW clouds breaking with shafts of light.
- **Extends:** PNW; nature metaphors.
- **Act II beat:** Coda — the first block is planted.
